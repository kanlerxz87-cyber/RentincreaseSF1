A clear path to a responsible rent increase

An anonymized, source-linked review for two San Francisco units in the Outer Sunset, ZIP 94122.

THE SHORT ANSWER

Yes, an individual owner must report.

For a covered rental, report to the Rent Board Housing Inventory and obtain a current rent-increase license for each tenant-occupied unit. Corporate ownership is not required. The license must be on file when an annual or banked increase takes effect; obtaining it before serving notice is the safer sequence, not the stated statutory timing requirement.

Ordinary annual and eligible banked increases do not require a separate approval petition. The official sources do not establish a separate requirement to file each ordinary notice with the Board. Different increases, including operating-cost or capital-improvement petitions, have different rules.
