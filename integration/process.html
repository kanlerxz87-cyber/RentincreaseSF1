Rent Board first. Then notice.

A practical order of operations for annual and banked increases—not a substitute for reviewing the actual tenancy documents.

Why raise rent?

The current annual allowance is a neutral, published baseline and can help cover ordinary operating costs. It is not necessary to invent a repair, expense, or market-comparison justification. A landlord can choose less than the maximum. Specific costs or capital work above the ordinary allowance involve different procedures; ordinary repair obligations remain.
