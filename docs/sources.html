Sources

Thirty-one sources, all government-published. Every table row elsewhere on this site points to one of them. Where no official source existed for a value, the value was left blank and recorded on the Flagged page instead of being estimated.

On authority. The Rent Board's own copy of the Rent Ordinance is "not the official record of the laws or regulations of the City and County of San Francisco" and is "provided for administrative and public convenience only" (S19). The City's official publisher of the Administrative Code is American Legal Publishing. State statutes are cited from the California Legislative Information site and federal statutes from the Office of the Law Revision Counsel; both are the official publication channels for the text quoted.

How the verification was done

  • Every percentage in the annual series was taken from the Rent Board's rate sheet, not from a summary article, and cross-checked against the Rent Board's announcement for the current year.
  • Every quotation on this site was read in the source page itself on 6 November 2026; quotations are reproduced with their subordinate clauses intact.
  • Every dollar figure was recomputed arithmetically from the official percentages and the base rent supplied. Nothing was rounded up and nothing was compounded.
  • Where two official sources conflicted, both are reproduced on the Flagged page and the more conservative figure is used for any recommendation.
  • Where no official source existed — market rent, owner-behaviour statistics, bedroom counts — no figure is published.
