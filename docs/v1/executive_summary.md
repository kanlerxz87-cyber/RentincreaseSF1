## Legal Framework & Allowable Increases

### 1. Local Law (San Francisco Rent Ordinance - Administrative Code Chapter 37)
- **Coverage:** Units built before June 13, 1979 (Building built 1940 qualifies) [1](https://cityrulelookup.com/rental-property/rent-control/san-francisco-ca)
- **Allowable Annual Increase (March 1, 2025 – February 28, 2026):** 1.4% [1](https://cityrulelookup.com/rental-property/rent-control/san-francisco-ca) [2](https://landlordos.net/learn/questions/sf-annual-rent-increase-2026)
- **Allowable Annual Increase (March 1, 2026 – February 28, 2027):** 1.6% [1](https://www.sf.gov/reports--current-rates-including-rent-increase-relocation-sec-deposit) [2](https://www.bancalsf.com/banked-rent-increases-san-francisco)
- **Calculation:** 60% of Bay Area CPI, capped at 7% maximum [2](https://landlordos.net/learn/questions/sf-annual-rent-increase-2026)
- **Banking:** Unused allowable increases can be banked and imposed later (subject to rules) [2](https://www.bancalsf.com/banked-rent-increases-san-francisco)
- **Notice Requirements:** 30 days for increases under 10%, 90 days for increases over 10% [2](https://landlordos.net/learn/questions/sf-annual-rent-increase-2026) [4](https://www.lifetimepm.com/blog/california-rent-increase-guide-2026)

### 2. State Law (California Tenant Protection Act - AB 1482)
- **Cap:** 5% + regional CPI, maximum 10% in any 12-month period [1](https://calandlordtenantlaws.com/rent-control-ab-1482) [3](https://www.lifetimepm.com/blog/california-rent-increase-guide-2026)
- **Applicability:** SF Rent Ordinance (local) overrides AB 1482 for covered units (pre-1979 buildings) [4](https://www.lifetimepm.com/blog/california-rent-increase-guide-2026) [5](https://www.leaserunner.com/blog/month-to-month-rent-increase-california)
- **Notice:** 30 days (≤10%), 90 days (>10%) [3](https://www.lifetimepm.com/blog/california-rent-increase-guide-2026)

### 3. Federal Law
- **No federal cap** on private market rent increases [1](https://www.fflw.org/blog/how-much-can-a-landlord-raise-rent) [5](https://www.lethub.co/blog/how-much-can-you-raise-rent)
- **Fair Housing Act:** Prohibits discrimination based on protected classes [1](https://www.fflw.org/blog/how-much-can-a-landlord-raise-rent)
- **HUD-assisted housing:** Subject to program rules (not applicable here as units are private) [1](https://www.fflw.org/blog/how-much-can-a-landlord-raise-rent)

## Corporate vs Individual Owner Practices

### Big Real Estate Corporations
- **Standardized processes:** Use automated systems to calculate increases based on Rent Board rates [3](https://christopherleesf.com/san-francisco-rent-control-laws-2025/)
- **Banking:** Frequently bank unused increases for future use [2](https://www.bancalsf.com/banked-rent-increases-san-francisco)
- **Compliance:** Maintain detailed records and file Housing Inventory reports [3](https://christopherleesf.com/san-francisco-rent-control-laws-2025/) [3](https://www.bancalsf.com/sf-rent-board-housing-inventory)
- **Legal Review:** Employ in-house counsel or specialized firms to review increases against SF Rent Board rules [3](https://christopherleesf.com/san-francisco-rent-control-laws-2025/)

### Individual Property Owners
- **Manual calculation:** Often rely on Rent Board calculators or third-party services [5](https://rentreboot.com/guide/bay-area-rent-control-by-city-2026)
- **Less banking:** May not bank increases due to administrative complexity [2](https://www.bancalsf.com/banked-rent-increases-san-francisco)
- **Direct communication:** More likely to communicate directly with tenants [3](https://christopherleesf.com/san-francisco-rent-control-laws-2025/)
- **Legal risk:** Higher risk of non-compliance if not using professional services [3](https://christopherleesf.com/san-francisco-rent-control-laws-2025/)

## Tenant Notification & Reasonable Reasons

### Correct Notification Method
- **Written notice:** Required (30 days for ≤10%, 90 days for >10%) [2](https://landlordos.net/learn/questions/sf-annual-rent-increase-2026) [4](https://www.lifetimepm.com/blog/california-rent-increase-guide-2026)
- **Content:** Must include new rent amount, effective date, and statement of rights [2](https://landlordos.net/learn/questions/sf-annual-rent-increase-2026)
- **Delivery:** Personal delivery, certified mail, or other methods specified in lease [4](https://www.lifetimepm.com/blog/california-rent-increase-guide-2026)

### Reasonable Reasons
- **Annual allowable increase:** Based on Rent Board rate (1.4% or 1.6%) [1](https://www.sf.gov/reports--current-rates-including-rent-increase-relocation-sec-deposit) [2](https://landlordos.net/learn/questions/sf-annual-rent-increase-2026)
- **Capital improvements:** Up to 10% with Rent Board approval [4](https://urbantaken.com/understanding-rent-control-in-san-francisco/)
- **Operating cost increases:** Up to 7% with Rent Board approval [4](https://urbantaken.com/understanding-rent-control-in-san-francisco/)
- **Utility cost pass-through:** With proper documentation [4](https://urbantaken.com/understanding-rent-control-in-san-francisco/)
