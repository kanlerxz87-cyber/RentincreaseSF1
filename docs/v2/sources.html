S04SF Rent Board (sf.gov)Banked rent increasesofficial-guidancePage fetched 2026-11-06. States a skipped increase is banked only if at least 24 full months pass without any rent increase; banking allowed since 4/1/1982; no limit; no compounding; notice must identify the banked portion.
S05SF Rent Board (sf.gov)Annual rent increase for multiple yearsofficial-guidancePage fetched 2026-11-06. Method: add the percentage for each year together (not compound); no petition needed; no rounding up.
S06SF Rent Board (sf.gov)Increasing rents during irregular periodsofficial-guidancePage fetched 2026-11-06. Covers increases 12-24 months and 24+ months from the anniversary date, and the trade-off of losing an annual increase when the date shifts.
S07SF Rent Board (sf.gov)Learn about San Francisco rental laws (June 13, 1979 coverage date)official-guidancePage fetched 2026-11-06. Units built on or before June 13, 1979 that are not single-family dwellings generally have rent control and eviction protection.
S08San Francisco Administrative Code Chapter 37 (Rent Ordinance)Sec. 37.3 - Rent Limitationsofficial-primaryOrdinance text fetched 2026-11-06. Contains the annual-increase formula (60% of published CPI, cap 7%), banking, notice content requirements per (b)(1)-(5), additional-occupants prohibition at (a)(11), and the null-and-void rule at (b)(5).
S09San Francisco Administrative Code Chapter 37 (Rent Ordinance)Sec. 37.15 - Reporting Obligations; Licensingofficial-primaryOrdinance text fetched 2026-11-06. A rent increase license is required; if the owner has not substantially complied, the license is suspended and the tenant need not pay the increase for the suspension period.
S10SF Rent Board (sf.gov)The Rent Board Housing Inventoryofficial-guidancePage fetched 2026-11-06. Annual deadline March 1; a rent increase notice is not enforceable without a license on the effective date of the notice; reporting began March 1, 2023 for buildings with fewer than 10 units.
S11San Francisco Administrative Code Chapter 37 (Rent Ordinance)Sec. 37.10B - Tenant Harassmentofficial-primaryOrdinance text fetched 2026-11-06. Includes (a)(5) on rent increases used to influence a tenant to vacate, with the three bad-faith factors, and (a)(9) on discrimination.
S12SF Rent Board (sf.gov)Landlord Petitions and Passthroughsofficial-guidancePage fetched 2026-11-06. Lists the six petition-based increases plus the passthroughs that use Rent Board worksheets; a petition must be filed before the notice is served.
S13SF Rent Board (sf.gov)Operating and Maintenance Expense Petitions (Topic 322)official-guidancePage fetched 2026-11-06. Up to 7% additional base rent; requires expenses to rise faster than the annual increase; petition must be filed before the notice; tenant hardship application available.
S14SF Rent Board (sf.gov)Special Circumstances Petitions Based on Rents for Comparable Units (Topic 323)official-guidancePage fetched 2026-11-06. Directly states that a long-standing tenant paying below market is NOT itself an extraordinary circumstance.
S15SF Rent Board (sf.gov)Algorithmic devices that set rent are prohibited in San Francisco (Topic 268)official-guidancePage fetched 2026-11-06. Admin Code Sec. 37.10C effective October 14, 2024; bans sale and use of revenue-management software that uses non-public competitor data to set rent or occupancy.
S16SF Rent Board (sf.gov)Charging for Additional Occupants Prohibited (Topics 152, 254)official-guidancePage fetched 2026-11-06. Charging more solely because an occupant was added is an unlawful rent increase even if the lease allows it.
S17SF Rent Board (sf.gov)Rent Increases Under Section 6.14 And Costa-Hawkins (Topic 153)official-guidancePage fetched 2026-11-06. A co-tenant is not subject to an unlimited increase regardless of when the co-tenant moved in; only a subtenant may be, and only with a timely 6.14 notice.

Sources that were specifically not relied on

Several commercial calculators and blogs publish 1.7% for March 2026–February 2027. The Rent Board's own pages and its official PDF both state 1.6%, and 1.7% is the rate for the previous year. Those sites appear to have carried forward the wrong year. See FLAG-11. S03 S19

The Census data API responded “missing key” to every query in this session, and the HUD Fair Market Rent interactive lookup returned an empty result for the available area parameters. No rent benchmark was taken from either, so no market figure appears anywhere on this site. See FLAG-05.
