Individual landlords frequently confuse the Rent Board's free Rent Increase License, the $59/unit Rent Board Fee, and the Treasurer's Business Registration Certificate.

2. Complete 8-Step Walkthrough for First-Time Individual Owners

Designed for an individual owner with zero prior experience navigating the SF Rent Board Portal (portal.sfrb.org).

Step Task Detailed Beginner Instructions Cost / Deadline Official Source
Step 1 Gather Basic Property & Tenancy Details (Keep Personal Tenant Info Internal) For each of the two units, note: (a) owner or designated habitability contact name, business mailing address, phone, and email; (b) Business Registration Number 'if any' (leave blank if exempt under the <4-unit residential structure rule); (c) approximate unit square footage in 250 sq. ft. increments (good-faith estimate permitted; check SF Property Information Map if unsure); (d) bedroom and bathroom count; (e) occupancy start date (Feb 1, 2025 for Unit 1; Jun 1, 2019 for Unit 2); and (f) current base rent in $250 increments plus which utilities (water/sewer, trash, gas, electricity) are included in base rent. $0 preparation; annual statutory deadline March 1 (late submissions accepted without monetary fine) San Francisco Rent Board (GS-02)Verbatim excerpt →
Step 2 Create or Log Into Your Property Owner Account at portal.sfrb.org Go to https://portal.sfrb.org/ and click 'Create an Account' (or 'Login') under Property Owners/Managers. Link your Outer Sunset (94122) parcel/APN using the PIN from your Rent Board mailed notice or request PIN/parcel linking through the portal or 311 (415-701-2311). For a 2-unit building, use the simple 'Individual Unit' web form — do NOT use the 5+ unit Excel bulk upload template. $0 / Available online 24/7 San Francisco Rent Board (GS-03)Verbatim excerpt →
Step 3 Submit the Housing Inventory for Unit 1 and Unit 2 Under Penalty of Perjury Select Unit 1 ('Non-Owner Occupied' / tenant-occupied), enter the required unit facts, review, and submit under penalty of perjury. Repeat for Unit 2. Note that Housing Inventory submissions are public record as to unit characteristics and owner business contact info (do not enter private tenant SSNs or irrelevant personal notes). Update owner/manager contact details within 30 days of any future change. $0 / Free online filing; generates license immediately upon submission San Francisco Rent Board (GS-01)Verbatim excerpt →
Step 4 Download Both Rent Increase Licenses & Verify Rent Board Fee ($59.00/Unit) Status Immediately after submitting the Housing Inventory for each tenant-occupied unit, confirm that the Portal shows an ACTIVE 'Rent Increase License' for Unit 1 and Unit 2 and save dated PDF/screenshot copies to your records. While in portal.sfrb.org, verify that the annual Rent Board Fee ($59.00 per dwelling unit for 2026–2027, due March 1) is paid in full so no delinquency or late penalty accrues. License: $0 | Rent Board Fee: $59.00/unit ($118.00 total for 2 units) due March 1 San Francisco Rent Board (GS-06)Verbatim excerpt →
Step 5 Verify Both Written Leases, Complete Rent Ledgers, Habitability, & Pending File #261020 Before calculating any increase: (1) check Unit 1's April 1, 2026 agreement to confirm whether it is month-to-month or a fixed-term lease (fixed-term leases bar mid-term increases unless expressly permitted); (2) audit Unit 2's full payment ledger since June 1, 2019 to confirm whether base rent is still $3,150.00 with zero intervening increases; (3) confirm zero unabated DBI/health citations (Civ. Code § 1942.4) and zero open repair requests (Form 516B deferral risk) or 180-day retaliation triggers (Civ. Code § 1942.5); and (4) check Legistar File #261020 if considering >10% banked increases. Mandatory pre-notice legal & factual gate California Department of Real Estate / Hosted by California Courts (GS-23)Verbatim excerpt →
Step 6 Compute Increase on Base Rent Only — Add Percentages (Never Compound, Never Round Up) Use the Form 571 rate in effect on the EFFECTIVE date of the increase (1.6% for effective dates March 1, 2026 – February 28, 2027). Multiply current base rent (excluding temporary utility/fee passthroughs) by the exact percentage. If adding banked years, sum the percentages first (never compound year-over-year) and never round up to the nearest dollar. 1.6% current annual rate (effective 2026-03-01 through 2027-02-28) San Francisco Rent Board (GS-08)Verbatim excerpt →
Step 7 Serve Itemized Written Notice via Personal Delivery or USPS Mail (Never Email Alone) Draft a clean written notice stating old base rent, dollar increase, percentage increase, new base rent, effective date, and an explicit breakdown of the annual portion vs. banked portion (with banked anniversary dates per Rule 4.12(b)). Serve EVERY adult tenant via (A) personal hand delivery (at least 30 days before effective date for <=10%, or 90 days for >10%), OR (B) USPS First-Class Mail within California (+5 calendar days = at least 35 days for <=10%, or 95 days for >10%, with date & place of mailing noted per CCP § 1013(b)). Keep signed proof of service. 30/35 days (<=10%) or 90/95 days (>10% in 12 months) California Legislative Information (GS-21)Verbatim excerpt →
Step 8 Re-Confirm Active License on the Effective Date & Renew Housing Inventory Every March 1 Because the March 1 annual Housing Inventory cycle falls right around late-February/March effective dates, log into portal.sfrb.org before March 1 each year to renew both units so your Rent Increase License never lapses on or after the effective date. Update your rent ledger with the new base rent and new anniversary date. Annual renewal by March 1 each year San Francisco Rent Board (GS-01)Verbatim excerpt →
