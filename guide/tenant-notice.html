1. Correct Statutory Protocol for Informing Tenants of a Rent Increase

Every mandatory content and service requirement under California Civil Code § 827, CCP § 1013, SF Admin. Code § 37.3, and Rent Board Rules §§ 4.10 & 4.12.

1. Written Form & Addressees Must be in writing and addressed to all adult tenants of record on the rental agreement (using neutral, professional business language). Cal. Civ. Code § 827(a)–(b) Never issue a rent increase orally, by text message, by email alone, or addressed to only one roommate. California Legislative Information (GS-21)Verbatim excerpt →

Itemization of Annual vs. Banked Portions & Basis Dates State separately which portion reflects the current annual increase and which portion reflects banked increases, listing each banked anniversary period/date. (Note: Rule 4.12(b) & Rule 4.10(b) state omitting banked dates does not void an otherwise within-cap increase, but full itemization prevents administrative hearings.) SF Rent Board Rules §§ 4.10(a)(1), 4.10(b), 4.12(b) Compounding banked percentages year-over-year instead of adding them, or lumping banked + annual without showing the breakdown. San Francisco Rent Board (GS-12)Verbatim excerpt →
5. Lawful Service Method & Lead-Time Calculation Serve ONLY by (A) personal hand delivery to each tenant (30 days if <=10%; 90 days if >10% cumulative in 12 months), OR (B) USPS First-Class Mail with postage paid (+5 calendar days within CA = 35 days if <=10%, 95 days if >10%; +10 days out-of-state; +20 days international) bearing a notation of date and place of mailing. Cal. Civ. Code § 827(b)(1)–(3); CCP § 1013(a)–(b) Posting on the door ('nail and mail') or emailing is NOT valid service under Civ. Code § 827(b)(1)! Build in 3–5 extra buffer days before the statutory deadline. California Legislative Information (GS-22)Verbatim excerpt →
6. Lawful & Reasonable Business Reason Statement No hardship excuse is needed for a Form 571 annual/banked adjustment. If stating a reason, cite the neutral statutory allowance ('Annual allowable rent adjustment pursuant to SF Admin. Code § 37.3(a) and Form 571 in connection with standard building operating, tax, insurance, and maintenance costs'). SF Admin. Code § 37.3(a)(1)–(2); Cal. Civ. Code § 1942.5(f)–(g) NEVER cite an added roommate (§ 37.3(a)(11)), repair costs requested by the tenant (§ 1942.5), or threats that the tenant should move out (§ 37.10B).
