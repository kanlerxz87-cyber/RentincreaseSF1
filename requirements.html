REQ-16StateStatewide cap (AB 1482) applicability
Does not control here

Reading the matrix

Local law does nearly all the work. State law supplies the notice periods and the anti-retaliation and anti-discrimination rules, and it supplies a rent cap that is deliberately displaced here. Federal law supplies no ceiling at all. If you only read three rows, read REQ-03 (frequency), REQ-06 (the 24-month banking rule) and REQ-09 (the licence).
