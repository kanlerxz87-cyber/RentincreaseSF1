Question 3 of 6 · telling the tenants

How to serve the increase, and what counts as a reason

A conforming percentage can still be void if the notice is late or incomplete. The notice must state the dollar amount, the percentage, the effective date, and which part of the increase is annual and which is banked — with the dates the banked part rests on.

30 / 90 days+5 if mailedItemise annual vs bankedNo rounding up

The notice is where increases failNot the percentage. A conforming amount in a nonconforming notice is void.

San Francisco treats notice defects strictly: §37.3(b)(5) provides that “any rent increase which does not conform with the provisions of this section shall be null and void”. Civil Code §827 separately sets the minimum notice period. Both must be satisfied, plus the licence, before the increase can be enforced.

No justification letter is required for the annual or banked portion. What is required is that the notice state what parts of the increase are annual and banked, and the dates the banked amount is based on.
