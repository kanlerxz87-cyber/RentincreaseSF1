Notice requirements, without overstating the consequencesUse the strict conservative content checklist; do not claim every omission has the same legal result.

Civil Code §827 sets written notice periods. Section 37.3(b)(5) states a null-and-void rule, while Rules 4.10(b) and 4.12(b) contain text-specific exceptions—including an express statement that omission of banked dates does not itself void the increase. The safe practice is to include every element rather than litigate an exception.
