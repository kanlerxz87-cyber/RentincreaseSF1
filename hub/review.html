The test, run honestlyA tenant lawyer's first question is procedural, not arithmetic.

Tenant-side review in San Francisco follows a predictable order: does the owner hold the licence, is the unit covered, is the percentage right, was the notice conforming and timely, and is there any sign of retaliation or discrimination. Most successful challenges fail an increase on the first or fourth question, which is why they are worth checking before the notice is served rather than after.

Twelve checks. Seven can be answered from this file; five need documents only you hold.
