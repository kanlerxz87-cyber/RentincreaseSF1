What “reasonable and normal” can mean hereA routine planning scenario is not an official market average or a legal clearance.

The simplest routine scenario is the published 1.6% annual allowance, applied only after the lease, ledger, anniversary, license, coverage, and notice checks. Valid banked amounts may lawfully exceed that current annual percentage. Other petition, passthrough, and vacancy routes have their own rules; this record does not establish them.
