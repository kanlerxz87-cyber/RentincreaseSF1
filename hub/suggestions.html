Suggestions — review every path before implementing anythingA dedicated review section: suggested operating steps, decisions not yet supported, and how to discover what is still unknown.

This section collects suggestions for how the owner should proceed and how this site can support better decisions. None of it is legal advice. Each suggested step carries its official basis where one exists; every unsupported private fact is left blank and flagged rather than assumed.

Choose a path before acting: raise neither unit until the flags are resolved; raise Unit 1 only (annual-only, once the lease instrument and licence are confirmed); raise Unit 2 only (annual-only once the current base is verified); or raise both on the same verified schedule. The site records the reasons for whichever path is chosen — neutral, documented differences reduce, but do not eliminate, discrimination or retaliation exposure.
