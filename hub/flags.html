H-01LOWBanking guidance uses two measuring points; worked-example labels need careRule 4.12(a) reads in full: "A landlord who refrains from imposing an annual rent increase, or any portion thereof, may accumulate said increase and impose that amount on or after the tenant's subsequent rent increase anniversary date; however, the rent may be increased only one time every twelve (12) months. This banked amount may only be given at the time of an annual increase. Only those increases which could have been imposed on, or subsequent to, April 1, 1982, may be accumulated. A full 12 months must have elapsed from the date that an annual rent increase, or a portion thereof, could have been imposed before this banking section becomes applicable. Banked increases shall not be compounded and shall not be rounded up". The Rent Board's banking guidance page uses a different measuring point: "A skipped increase is only banked if at least 24 full months pass without any rent increase." Re-fetched 2026-11-08. Effect: The 1.4% component is not conditional on choosing between two rules. It remains conditional on the actual lease, anniversary, license, and ledger.
