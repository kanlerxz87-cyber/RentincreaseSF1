Three severities. High items can change an amount or make an increase unenforceable. Medium items affect documentation or the calendar. Low items are judgement calls or repository defects. Every row links to the official source it concerns, and where an official value is missing the value is left blank elsewhere on the site rather than inferred.

Read H-01 first. Two official Rent Board publications describe different banking look-back tests — 24 months on the “Banked rent increases” page, 12 months in Rule 4.12(a) and in the worked examples on “Learn about rent increases”. The Unit 1 figure of $119.85 includes a 1.4% banked portion that depends on which reading applies; the figure that does not depend on it is $63.92.
