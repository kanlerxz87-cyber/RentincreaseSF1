Bottom line. For a covered, licensed unit, the ordinary and lawful annual increase effective March 1, 2026 through February 28, 2027 is 1.6% of base rent, once every 12 months, with 30 days' written notice (90 days if the increase exceeds 10%, plus 5 days if mailed). S02 S01 S29 The state AB 1482 cap of 8.8% for the San Francisco area does not apply to units already subject to the more restrictive SF Rent Ordinance cap. S27 S28 No federal numeric rent cap for private unsubsidized housing was confirmed, so that value is left blank and flagged. S39 S37

Scrutiny standard. Any increase can be challenged by a tenant at no charge and with no time limit before a Rent Board Administrative Law Judge; free Rent Board counseling and an official referral listing of tenant legal resources exist. S18 S23 S22 Therefore the defensible approach is a documented, uniformly applied rule tied to each unit's own lawful base rent, anniversary date, and banking history — not to tenant characteristics. Important findings

  • Coverage is conditional. Official guidance says many units built on or before June 13, 1979 have rent control, but the specific certificate of occupancy and exemptions must be checked. S07
  • Unit A has an occupancy-change issue. A new or additional roommate cannot itself justify extra rent; an unlimited market-rate increase is available only in narrow 6.14 / Costa-Hawkins situations, and §1954.53(d)(3) does not apply where an original occupant remains. The City strongly recommends legal advice. S21 S19 S20 S32
  • Unit B may have banked increases. Skipped annual increases can be banked under detailed conditions, but eligibility depends on the full rent history and a 24-month rule; no case-specific banked amount is asserted as verified. S05 S06 S18
  • Prerequisites matter. A current rent increase license must be on file before an annual or banked increase takes effect. S13
  • No algorithmic rent setting. SF prohibits any landlord from using algorithmic devices fed by non-public competitor data to set rents; California and federal enforcers have acted against shared pricing algorithms. S10 S34 S35 S36
