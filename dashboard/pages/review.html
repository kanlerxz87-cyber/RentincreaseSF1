Tenant Lawyer Review

A checklist built from official sources for scrutiny by SF tenant advocates and lawyers.

Design standard

The project asks that any increase be able to pass review by San Francisco tenant-friendly advocates and lawyers, be considered reasonable and normal business practice, and show no discrimination or broken laws. The checklist below turns each official requirement into a review item. It is a compliance aid, not a guarantee of outcome and not legal advice. S18 S22 S33

Scope of free counsel

Proposition F / Tenant Right to Counsel is officially described for eviction proceedings; no official source was found guaranteeing free counsel for a rent-increase dispute. Free general counseling and clinic referrals are separately listed by the Rent Board. See F13. S25 S22 S23

What a tenant can challenge

A tenant can file an unlawful-rent-increase petition alleging that the increase exceeds the allowable annual/banked amount, is less than 12 months after the prior increase, or the landlord is not licensed. S18 S41
