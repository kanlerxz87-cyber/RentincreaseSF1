What must be resolved before any increase

  • Unit A: whether the occupancy change is a continuing tenancy with an original occupant (so the ordinary AGA applies) or a new tenancy (initial rent rules). The City describes 6.14 / Costa-Hawkins as complicated and strongly encourages legal advice. S19 S20 S32
  • Unit A: the lawful base rent, last increase date, and anniversary date; changing an anniversary date can permanently lose a skipped increase. S01 S05 S06
  • Unit B: the full rent history, because banked increases cannot be computed from a start date alone. S05 S18
  • Both: license status, prior notices, petitions, passthroughs, and any subsidy or exemption. S13 S14 S27
