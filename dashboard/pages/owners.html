What can and cannot be said from official sources

Official government material documents enforcement actions and rules, not a statistical description of "how corporations normally raise rent" versus "how individual owners normally raise rent". Practical conclusion (analysis, not legal advice)

The SF Rent Ordinance, the licensing requirement, the notice rules, and the algorithmic-device ban apply to "landlords" generally, not only to corporations. A small owner should therefore use the same official rate calculation, the same written notice, and the same documented non-discriminatory policy as a large owner. S09 S13 S10 S01
