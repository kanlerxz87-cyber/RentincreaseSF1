Bottom line: If the license is active, the rate is 1.6% (or a fully itemised additive banked amount), the notice is 30/90 days with +5 for mail, no extra charge is made for the new occupant, and no protected activity is in the 180-day window, the increase is the same kind of increase the Rent Board expects to see across the city, and a tenant lawyer will generally not find a viable challenge.
