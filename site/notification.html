Correct Notification Method

Legal Requirements (San Francisco Rent Ordinance)

  • Written Notice: Required for all rent increases [2](https://landlordos.net/learn/questions/sf-annual-rent-increase-2026) [4](https://www.lifetimepm.com/blog/california-rent-increase-guide-2026)
  • Notice Period:
    • 30 days for increases under 10% [2](https://landlordos.net/learn/questions/sf-annual-rent-increase-2026) [4](https://www.lifetimepm.com/blog/california-rent-increase-guide-2026)
    • 90 days for increases over 10% [2](https://landlordos.net/learn/questions/sf-annual-rent-increase-2026) [4](https://www.lifetimepm.com/blog/california-rent-increase-guide-2026)
  • Content Requirements:
    • New rent amount [2](https://landlordos.net/learn/questions/sf-annual-rent-increase-2026)
    • Effective date [2](https://landlordos.net/learn/questions/sf-annual-rent-increase-2026)
    • Statement of tenant rights [2](https://landlordos.net/learn/questions/sf-annual-rent-increase-2026)
    • Reference to SF Rent Ordinance [2](https://landlordos.net/learn/questions/sf-annual-rent-increase-2026)
  • Delivery Methods:
    • Personal delivery [4](https://www.lifetimepm.com/blog/california-rent-increase-guide-2026)
    • Certified mail [4](https://www.lifetimepm.com/blog/california-rent-increase-guide-2026)
    • Other methods specified in lease [4](https://www.lifetimepm.com/blog/california-rent-increase-guide-2026)

State Law (California AB 1482)

  • Notice Period: Same as local law (30 days for ≤10%, 90 days for >10%) [3](https://www.lifetimepm.com/blog/california-rent-increase-guide-2026)
  • Content: Must include statutory language for exempt properties [1](https://calandlordtenantlaws.com/rent-control-ab-1482)

Federal Law

  • Fair Housing Act: Notice must not discriminate based on protected classes [1](https://www.fflw.org/blog/how-much-can-a-landlord-raise-rent)

Reasonable Reasons for Rent Increase

1. Annual Allowable Increase (SF Rent Ordinance)

  • Basis: 60% of Bay Area CPI, capped at 7% [2](https://landlordos.net/learn/questions/sf-annual-rent-increase-2026)
  • 2025-26 Rate: 1.4% [1](https://cityrulelookup.com/rental-property/rent-control/san-francisco-ca) [2](https://landlordos.net/learn/questions/sf-annual-rent-increase-2026)
  • 2026-27 Rate: 1.6% [1](https://www.sf.gov/reports--current-rates-including-rent-increase-relocation-sec-deposit) [2](https://www.bancalsf.com/banked-rent-increases-san-francisco)
  • Banking: Unused increases can be accumulated and applied later [2](https://www.bancalsf.com/banked-rent-increases-san-francisco)

2. Capital Improvements

  • Maximum Increase: Up to 10% with SF Rent Board approval [4](https://urbantaken.com/understanding-rent-control-in-san-francisco/)
  • Requirements: Must document improvements and obtain approval [4](https://urbantaken.com/understanding-rent-control-in-san-francisco/)

3. Operating and Maintenance Costs

  • Maximum Increase: Up to 7% beyond annual limit with SF Rent Board approval [4](https://urbantaken.com/understanding-rent-control-in-san-francisco/)
  • Requirements: Must document cost increases and obtain approval [4](https://urbantaken.com/understanding-rent-control-in-san-francisco/)

4. Utility Cost Pass-Through

  • Requirements: Must document utility cost increases and provide proper notice [4](https://urbantaken.com/understanding-rent-control-in-san-francisco/)

5. State Law (AB 1482)

  • Cap: 5% + regional CPI, maximum 10% [1](https://calandlordtenantlaws.com/rent-control-ab-1482) [3](https://www.lifetimepm.com/blog/california-rent-increase-guide-2026)
  • Applicability: Only for non-covered units (SF Rent Ordinance overrides for pre-1979 buildings) [4](https://www.lifetimepm.com/blog/california-rent-increase-guide-2026) [5](https://www.leaserunner.com/blog/month-to-month-rent-increase-california)

Tenant Lawyer Scrutiny (SF Tenant Friendly Advocates)

Review Criteria

  • Compliance with SF Rent Ordinance: Must follow 1.4% (2025-26) or 1.6% (2026-27) rates [1](https://cityrulelookup.com/rental-property/rent-control/san-francisco-ca) [2](https://www.sf.gov/reports--current-rates-including-rent-increase-relocation-sec-deposit)
  • Notice Compliance: Must provide 30/90 days written notice [2](https://landlordos.net/learn/questions/sf-annual-rent-increase-2026) [4](https://www.lifetimepm.com/blog/california-rent-increase-guide-2026)
  • Non-Discrimination: Must not discriminate based on protected classes (Fair Housing Act) [1](https://www.fflw.org/blog/how-much-can-a-landlord-raise-rent)
  • Banking Documentation: Must document banked increases [2](https://www.bancalsf.com/banked-rent-increases-san-francisco)
  • Capital Improvement Approval: Must have SF Rent Board approval for >1.6% increases [4](https://urbantaken.com/understanding-rent-control-in-san-francisco/)

Potential Issues

  • Selective Increases: Raising rent on one unit but not another could trigger discrimination claims [1](https://www.fflw.org/blog/how-much-can-a-landlord-raise-rent)
  • Insufficient Notice: Less than 30/90 days notice invalidates increase [2](https://landlordos.net/learn/questions/sf-annual-rent-increase-2026) [4](https://www.lifetimepm.com/blog/california-rent-increase-guide-2026)
  • Exceeding Cap: Increases above 1.6% (2026-27) without approval are void [2](https://landlordos.net/learn/questions/sf-annual-rent-increase-2026) [4](https://urbantaken.com/understanding-rent-control-in-san-francisco/)
