Corporate vs Individual Owner Practices

Analysis Date: November 6, 2026

Big Real Estate Corporations

Standardized Processes

  • Automated Calculation: Use software systems to calculate increases based on SF Rent Board rates (1.4% for 2025-26, 1.6% for 2026-27) [1](https://cityrulelookup.com/rental-property/rent-control/san-francisco-ca) [2](https://www.sf.gov/reports--current-rates-including-rent-increase-relocation-sec-deposit)
  • Banking Management: Systematically track and apply banked increases (unused allowable percentages from previous years) [2](https://www.bancalsf.com/banked-rent-increases-san-francisco)
  • Compliance Tracking: Maintain detailed records for SF Rent Board Housing Inventory reporting (due March 1) [3](https://www.bancalsf.com/sf-rent-board-housing-inventory)
  • Legal Review: Employ in-house counsel or specialized firms to review increases against SF Rent Board rules and Fair Housing Act [1](https://www.fflw.org/blog/how-much-can-a-landlord-raise-rent) [3](https://christopherleesf.com/san-francisco-rent-control-laws-2025/)
  • Notice Automation: Generate and deliver written notices (30 days for ≤10%, 90 days for >10%) automatically [2](https://landlordos.net/learn/questions/sf-annual-rent-increase-2026) [4](https://www.lifetimepm.com/blog/california-rent-increase-guide-2026)

Practices Observed

  • Consistent Application: Apply the same percentage increase across all units to avoid discrimination claims [1](https://www.fflw.org/blog/how-much-can-a-landlord-raise-rent)
  • Capital Improvement Petitions: Regularly petition for additional increases (up to 10%) with Rent Board approval [4](https://urbantaken.com/understanding-rent-control-in-san-francisco/)
  • Operating Cost Pass-Throughs: Document and apply operating cost increases (up to 7%) with approval [4](https://urbantaken.com/understanding-rent-control-in-san-francisco/)
  • Utility Cost Pass-Throughs: Pass on utility cost increases with proper documentation [4](https://urbantaken.com/understanding-rent-control-in-san-francisco/)

Individual Property Owners

Manual Processes

  • Calculation: Often rely on SF Rent Board calculators or third-party services [5](https://rentreboot.com/guide/bay-area-rent-control-by-city-2026)
  • Banking: Less likely to bank increases due to administrative complexity [2](https://www.bancalsf.com/banked-rent-increases-san-francisco)
  • Compliance: May struggle with Housing Inventory reporting and Rent Board fees ($59 per unit) [3](https://www.bancalsf.com/sf-rent-board-housing-inventory)
  • Legal Risk: Higher risk of non-compliance if not using professional services [3](https://christopherleesf.com/san-francisco-rent-control-laws-2025/)

Practices Observed

  • Direct Communication: More likely to communicate directly with tenants [3](https://christopherleesf.com/san-francisco-rent-control-laws-2025/)
  • Selective Increases: May raise rent on one unit but not another (higher discrimination risk) [1](https://www.fflw.org/blog/how-much-can-a-landlord-raise-rent)
  • Limited Banking: Rarely bank increases due to complexity [2](https://www.bancalsf.com/banked-rent-increases-san-francisco)
  • Notice Delivery: May use less formal methods (risk of non-compliance) [4](https://www.lifetimepm.com/blog/california-rent-increase-guide-2026)
