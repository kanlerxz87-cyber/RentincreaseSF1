What this is

A line-by-line, source-linked answer to one question: what is a reasonable and normal rent increase for two rent-controlled units in a 1940 building in San Francisco's Outer Sunset, ZIP 94122?

The answer is not a matter of judgement. In San Francisco the ceiling is arithmetic: the Rent Board's published annual percentage, plus any annual increases the owner skipped and is still entitled to bank. Both units sit well below the statewide cap and no federal law sets a rent ceiling at all, so the local ordinance decides everything.

The single most important finding

The two units have very different lawful ceilings — 3.0% and 13.1% — not because the units differ, but because each tenancy has its own history of skipped increases. Unit 1's tenancy began in 2025; Unit 2's began in 2019 and has skipped six consecutive annual increases. That difference is lawful, explainable and documented, and it is the reason a single uniform percentage is not the right approach.
