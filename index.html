Answer in one paragraph

Both units sit in a building constructed in 1940, so they are presumed covered by the San Francisco Rent Ordinance. For a covered unit the “reasonable and normal” increase is not a matter of market judgement: it is the Rent Board's published annual allowable increase — 1.6% for increases taking effect between 1 March 2026 and 28 February 2027 — applied once every twelve months to the lawful base rent, plus any properly banked prior-year allowances that were never imposed S01 S02 S03. Anything above that figure requires a Rent Board petition and evidence of the kind of cost increase the Ordinance recognises S10. Adding a roommate is never a lawful reason to raise rent on a continuing tenancy S08.
