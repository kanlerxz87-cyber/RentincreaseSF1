Start here — the individual-owner Rent Board answerThe legal gate is licensure on the effective date. The safer operating sequence is to finish reporting before serving notice.

Yes. Each tenant-occupied unit needs a current Rent Increase License before an annual or banked increase takes effect — even when the owner is one individual with no corporation.

San Francisco Administrative Code §37.15 contains no individual-owner or two-unit exemption. Report each unit through the annual Housing Inventory; after substantial reporting compliance, the Rent Board automatically generates the unit's license. Reporting is free. There is no separate per-increase approval application, and an ordinary annual/banked notice goes to the tenant, not to the Rent Board.

Timing, precisely: the official hard cutoff is a current license on the increase's effective date, not necessarily the day the notice is served. Best practice is to complete/update reporting and confirm the license before serving notice so a correctable omission does not become an unenforceable increase. Late reporting is allowed, but restoration is prospective; the tenant does not owe the increase for unlicensed suspension months. The license does not have to be attached to the notice.

Two different City registrations are often confused — only one gates the rent increase. The Rent Increase License comes from the Rent Board's Housing Inventory and is what makes an annual or banked increase enforceable. A Business Registration Certificate comes from the Office of the Treasurer & Tax Collector as a general business-tax registration; do not treat one registration as satisfying the other. Whether this owner needs one is not established: rental income from "one residential structure consisting of fewer than four units" is not, "by reason of that fact alone", required to register, but the brief supplies two rent-controlled units, not the total structure unit count or other business activity. Other San Francisco business activity, payroll expense, or short-term rental income can trigger registration. Confirm the facts before relying on the exception S24 S25 S42; logged as H-21.

Use this order: confirm coverage and lease terms → reconcile the full rent ledger → submit/update the Housing Inventory and confirm both licenses → calculate the amount → serve a conforming written notice → verify the license remains current on the effective date and retain proof.
