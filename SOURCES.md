## Source cautions

- **S04 / S07:** banking guidance and Rule 4.12 use different measuring points; read together, not as a proven conflict.
- **S05 / S04:** official worked-example year labels contain errors; Form 571 (S06) controls periods and rates.
- **S24 / S25 / S42:** two reported rent-controlled units do not establish the total structure unit count or a settled business-registration exemption.
- **S26:** File #261020 is pending, not law; re-check before any large banked catch-up.
- **S27 / S28 / S29:** occupant-change reset rules are preserved from PR #20; a reset is keyed to the last original occupant leaving, and the §6.14 notice/co-tenant facts remain open.
- **S32 / S33:** bond passthrough requires actual tenancy commencement, net taxable value, unit count, and a completed worksheet; no dollar amount is inferred. The worksheet’s license phase-in dates conflict with Rule 13.14/§37.15 (H-26).
- **S41 / S08:** the multiple-year page’s 30/35-day shorthand conflicts with Civil Code §827’s 90-day band for >10% increases (H-27).
- **S02 / S44 / S38:** Ordinance 3-26 effective date differs between the Board convenience compilation and the official publisher (H-28).
- **S22:** DOJ allegations/proposed settlement are not universal corporate behavior or a final adjudication.
- **S19:** a referral listing is not a guarantee of free representation.

Owner-brief dates and amounts are not official facts. They remain labeled as scenario inputs throughout the canonical site.
