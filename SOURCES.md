## Tiers

- **official-primary** — Statute, ordinance, regulation, or government agency page. Primary authority.
- **official-guidance** — Government agency guidance/explainer page. Authoritative interpretation, not the statute text.
- **official-org** — Official website of the organization named (e.g. the legal-aid provider itself). Not a government source.
- **secondary** — Commercial or practitioner source. Used only for context and always flagged. Never used as the basis for a value.

## Deliberately not relied on

- Several commercial rent calculators publish **1.7%** for 3/1/26–2/28/27. The Rent Board's own pages and its official PDF both state **1.6%**; 1.7% is the rate for 3/1/24–2/28/25. See FLAG-11.
- The U.S. Census data API returned "missing key" for every query this session, and the HUD Fair Market Rent interactive lookup returned an empty result for the available area parameters. No rent benchmark was taken from either, so no market figure appears anywhere on the site. See FLAG-05.
