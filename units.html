Facts of record

  • Base rent of record: $3,995.00 per month from 1 February 2025.
  • Three tenants named on the initial lease (anonymised as Tenant A-1, A-2, A-3).
  • Current occupancy: Tenant A-1 continues; Tenant A-4 added; the rent line dated 1 April 2026 remains $3,995.00.
  • At least one tenant from the original lease remains in possession, which on its face makes this a continuing tenancy rather than a new tenancy (see F03).

What follows legally

  • Because the tenancy began 1 February 2025, the first annual increase may take effect no earlier than 1 February 2026 (S01).
  • Adding Tenant A-4 cannot itself justify any rent increase, even if a lease clause says otherwise (S01, S08 §37.3(a)(11)).
  • The allowable annual increase in effect on any effective date between 1 March 2026 and 28 February 2027 is 1.6% (S01, S02, S03).
  • No banked amount is available yet. The 1.4% that could first have been imposed on 1 February 2026 banks only after a full 12 months have elapsed from that date — i.e. from 1 February 2027 under Rule 4.12 (S07), or 1 February 2028 under the 24-month wording on S04 (see F01).

What follows legally

  • If no increase has been imposed since 1 June 2019, seven annual increases have become available on 1 June of 2020–2026 and most of them are banked (S04, S07).
  • Any single increase greater than 10% of the rent charged in the preceding 12 months needs 90 days' written notice, not 30 (S15, S01).
  • An above-allowable increase would require a Rent Board petition; the fact that a long-standing tenant pays below market is expressly not a sufficient ground (S12).
