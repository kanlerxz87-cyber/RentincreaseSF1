SF Admin. Code § 37.15 (Reporting & Rent Increase License)		Sec. 37.15 — Reporting Obligations; Licensing (S.F. Administrative Code Ch. 37)
San Francisco Rent Board (sf.gov) · Local — Enacted Ordinance
Retrieved: 2026-11-11		“(a) Owners of residential dwelling units subject to this Chapter 37 shall be required to report certain information about their units to the Rent Board ...
