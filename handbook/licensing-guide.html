Licence & step-by-step

Rent Board licence: the individual-owner walkthrough

Free, online, and required. Follow the ten steps in order; keep dated Portal evidence for each unit.

The one-sentence answer

An individual owner must complete the free annual Housing Inventory report and have a current Rent Increase License for the unit on the effective date — and should finish reporting and save both licence PDFs before serving notice, because that is what prevents a Form 518/516C challenge.
