Notice & lawful reasons

How to notify tenants — and what counts as a lawful reason

Written notice, statutory service, complete itemisation, and no invented justifications.

Statutory service bands (Civil Code §827 + CCP §1013)

The 10% threshold counts the proposed increase together with other increases in the 12 months before the effective date. When in doubt, use the longer band and serve early.

File the paperwork, keep the proof

Keep the final signed notice, the calculation worksheet, the licence PDFs and the proof of service/certificate of mailing together per unit. If any certified, passthrough or petition component is ever added, file the petition/worksheet before serving that notice.
