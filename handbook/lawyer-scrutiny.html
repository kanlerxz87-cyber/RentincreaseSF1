Tenant-lawyer audit

Would this survive tenant-advocate scrutiny?

Sixteen gates, five petition routes, and the exact wording that helps or hurts.

Assume a tenant advocate reads every line

Tenants need no lawyer to petition, and the Rent Board referral listing includes counseling/legal-aid providers serving 94122 (including a Sunset-district office) — subject to each provider’s eligibility, scope and capacity. “Free representation for every notice review” is not guaranteed (FL-20). The defence is not rhetoric; it is passing all sixteen gates on documents.
