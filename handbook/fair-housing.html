Fair housing

Raising one unit or both: discrimination analysis

Motive and method decide legality. Document neutral criteria before you serve — especially with no small-owner exemption under California/SF law.

Short answer

Raising one unit and not the other is not automatically discrimination. Exposure turns on why that unit was chosen. Fix a neutral written policy from records (lease eligibility, proved base, anniversary, Form 571 rate, proved bank) before serving, apply the same method to both units, and defer any unit that fails a legal gate. For a two-unit owner there is no California/SF small-owner shelter to fall back on.
