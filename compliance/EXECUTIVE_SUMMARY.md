## 1. Forefront Priority — Does an Individual Property Owner (Not a Corporation) Need a Rent Board License Before Notifying Tenants?

### Short Answer: **YES for the SF Rent Board Rent Increase License — NO Incorporation Required.**

1. **Mandatory Individual-Owner Reporting & License (`SF Admin. Code §37.15`):**
   Every owner of residential rent-controlled units in San Francisco — **including a single individual (natural person) owning a 2-unit 1940 building** — must report unit information annually into the **SF Rent Board Housing Inventory** ([SF Admin. Code §37.15](https://www.sf.gov/information/sec-3715-reporting-obligations-licensing); [SF Rent Board Housing Inventory](https://www.sf.gov/rent-board-housing-inventory)). Buildings with 1–9 units became subject on **March 1, 2023**, with annual updates due every **March 1**. There is **no exemption** for individual, mom-and-pop, or non-corporate owners.
2. **How the License Works (Free, Online, Automatic — Not a Discretionary Permit):**
   You do **not** file a paper license application or wait for a Rent Board hearing. Reporting is **free** and available 24/7 on the [SF Rent Board Portal (`portal.sfrb.org`)](https://portal.sfrb.org/). Once you report a unit as tenant-occupied under penalty of perjury, the Portal **automatically generates your Rent Increase License**.
3. **Exact Timing Gate — Before Notice vs. Before Effective Date (So It Cannot Be Challenged):**
   - **Statutory Hard Gate:** Under [§37.15(e)](https://www.sf.gov/information/sec-3715-reporting-obligations-licensing) and [Housing Inventory guidance](https://www.sf.gov/rent-board-housing-inventory), a rent-increase notice is **unenforceable unless a current Rent Increase License is on file on the effective date** of the increase. During any period of reporting noncompliance, your license is **temporarily suspended**; filing later restores the license **prospectively only**, and tenants **never owe** the increased rent for any month during suspension.
   - **Best-Practice Sequence (Complete BEFORE Serving Notice):** Even though the statutory bar triggers on the effective date and you do **not** have to attach the license to the tenant notice, **complete the Housing Inventory for both units and save dated PDF/screenshot proof of both active licenses BEFORE serving any notice**. Housing Inventory submissions are **public**, and tenants or tenant counselors can look up your unit on [`portal.sfrb.org`](https://portal.sfrb.org/) the day they receive your notice.
4. **Do You Send the Rent Increase Notice to the Rent Board or Ask Permission First?**
   - **No** for lawful annual CPI (`1.6%`) or properly banked rent increases ([SF.gov Banked Rent Increases](https://www.sf.gov/information--banked-rent-increases); [SF Admin. Code §37.3(a)(1)–(2)](https://www.sf.gov/information--sec-373-rent-limitations)). Notices go to the tenants, not the Rent Board. Rent Board pre-filing is required **only** for petition-based increases above the annual/banked allowance (e.g., Capital Improvement, Operating & Maintenance, Comparable Rents, or Utility Passthrough worksheets — [Topic 301](https://www.sf.gov/landlord-petitions-and-passthroughs)).
5. **Do Not Confuse Three Separate City Requirements:**
   - **SF Rent Increase License (Rent Board):** **REQUIRED** for both tenant-occupied units; **$0 (free)** via [`portal.sfrb.org`](https://portal.sfrb.org/). Directly governs rent-increase enforceability ([§37.15](https://www.sf.gov/information/sec-3715-reporting-obligations-licensing)).
   - **SF Rent Board Fee (Rent Board):** **REQUIRED** unless exempt; **$59.00 per dwelling unit** for `2026–2027` (due March 1; 2027 fee exemption window closes **December 14, 2026**). Once paid in full, **50% (`$29.50`)** may be billed **separately** to tenants in occupancy as of November 1 (never added into base rent; late penalties never recoverable) ([SF Rent Board Fee](https://www.sf.gov/rent-board-fee)).
   - **SF Business Registration Certificate (Treasurer & Tax Collector):** **SEPARATE TAX REGISTRATION**; has **no effect** on Rent Ordinance rent-increase validity. On the reported facts (one residential building with **2 units**, i.e., fewer than 4 units), SF Treasurer guidance states a lessor of *"one residential structure consisting of fewer than four units"* is **not, by reason of that fact alone, required to register** or file a Gross Receipts Tax return — **conditionally** (other SF business activity, payroll expense, or any short-term rental income requires registration) ([SF Treasurer Lessor Guidance](https://sftreasurer.org/information-lessors-residential-real-estate); [Register a Business](https://sftreasurer.org/business/register-business)).

## 2. Reasonable & Normal Rent Increases in Outer Sunset 94122 — Local, State & Federal Law

- **Local Law Controls (`SF Admin. Code §37.3` + `Form 571`):** For a 1940 two-unit building (Certificate of Occupancy on or before `June 13, 1979`, not a separately alienable single-family home/condo), the SF Rent Ordinance controls. The published annual allowable increase effective **March 1, 2026 through February 28, 2027** is **1.6%** (`60%` of Bay Area CPI-U, capped at `7.0%`) ([Form 571 PDF](https://www.sf.gov/documents/34344/571_Allowable_Annual_Increases_26-27_1.pdf)). The `3/1/2027–2/29/2028` rate is not yet published as of `2026-11-10` (left blank and flagged).
- **No Official ZIP-94122 Market Norm:** No government agency publishes a lawful or "normal" rent-increase percentage specific to ZIP `94122`; commercial listing blogs are excluded and that confirmation field is left blank on `compliance/flagged.html`.
- **State Law (`AB 1482` & `Costa-Hawkins`):** California's AB 1482 rate in SF (`8.8%` for `8/1/2026–7/31/2027`) **does not apply** to units governed by SF's stricter local Rent Ordinance ([Cal. Civ. Code §1947.12(d)(3)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CIV&sectionNum=1947.12); [SF AB 1482 Report](https://www.sf.gov/reports--california-tenant-protection-act-2019-ab-1482)). Under Costa-Hawkins ([Cal. Civ. Code §1954.53(d)(3)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CIV&sectionNum=1954.53)) and [SF Rules §§6.13–6.14](https://www.sf.gov/reports--december-2022--rent-board-rules-and-regulations-part-vi-rent-increase-justifications), **Unit 1's April 1, 2026 partial occupant change does NOT allow a market-rate reset or roommate surcharge** because `Placeholder Tenant 1A` (an original Feb. 1, 2025 tenant) remains in lawful possession.
- **Federal Law:** The Federal Fair Housing Act ([42 U.S.C. §3604](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2023-title42/html/USCODE-2023-title42-chap45-subchapI-sec3604.htm)) prohibits discriminatory rent terms; no federal statute sets a percentage rent cap for private unsubsidized rentals.
- **CRITICAL NOV. 9, 2026 LEGISLATIVE ALERT (`File No. 261020` — Pending, NOT Enacted Law):** On **November 9, 2026**, the SF Rent Board posted an official alert on [Learn About Rent Increases](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-francisco) and [Banked Rent Increases](https://www.sf.gov/information--banked-rent-increases) noting that Board of Supervisors [File No. 261020](https://sfgov.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?ID=8217304&GUID=7EDA4662-EBFB-4AF5-962F-C669B64AA770) (`Pending Committee Action` on `2026-11-10` — **not law**), if enacted as currently drafted ([Legislative Digest Ver. 1](https://sfgov.legistar.com/View.ashx?M=F&ID=15865647&GUID=CC09B796-4675-4A7D-9960-85FE239F7FBB)), would cap combined **Banked + Capital Improvement** increases at **10% per year** of the lowest prior-12-month base rent through **December 31, 2029**, **retroactively covering banked-increase notices served on or after September 15, 2026** (while preserving excess banked percentages for **January 1, 2030+**).

## 3. Summary Answers to Inquiries 2–5

1. **Corporate Landlords vs. Individual SF Owners ([`/compliance/practices.html`](practices.html)):**
   Both face identical SF statutory obligations (`§§37.3, 37.10B, 37.10C, 37.15`). Nationally, 1–4 unit properties represent **97.5% of rental properties** (`82.9%` 1-unit + `14.6%` 2–4 unit) vs. **0.4%** for 50+ unit properties ([2024 HUD/Census RHFS](https://www.census.gov/content/dam/Census/programs-surveys/rhfs/2024-rhfs-infographic.pdf)). San Francisco prohibits algorithmic rent-setting software using nonpublic competitor data for **all** landlords ([SF Admin. Code §37.10C](https://www.sf.gov/information--sec-3710c-use-and-sale-algorithmic-devices-prohibited)), paralleling federal antitrust enforcement ([U.S. DOJ RealPage Proposed Settlement, Nov. 24, 2025](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/justice-department-requires-realpage-end-sharing-competitively-sensitive-information-and)). No official `.gov` dataset measures SF owner-type increase frequency (left blank & flagged).
2. **Proper Tenant Notification & Lawful Reasons ([`/compliance/notice-and-reasons.html`](notice-and-reasons.html)):**
   Serve **in writing** via **personal delivery** or **USPS first-class mail (`+5` calendar days in CA)** — never email/text/door-posting alone ([Cal. Civ. Code §827](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CIV&sectionNum=827); [CCP §1013(a)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CCP&sectionNum=1013)). State current base rent, dollar increase, percentage increase, new base rent, effective date, and exact annual vs. banked breakdown with banked basis dates ([§37.3(b)](https://www.sf.gov/information--sec-373-rent-limitations); [Rules §§4.10, 4.12](https://www.sf.gov/reports--december-2022--rent-board-rules-and-regulations-part-iv-rent-increases-not-requiring)). Lawful reasons are the **published annual CPI allowance** and **documented banked allowances** — never "market catch-up" or "additional roommate."
3. **Passing SF Tenant-Advocate & Free Tenant-Lawyer Scrutiny ([`/compliance/lawyer-scrutiny.html`](lawyer-scrutiny.html)):**
   Tenants in 94122 can petition the Rent Board **without a lawyer** or seek counseling from organizations on the [SF Rent Board Referral Listing](https://www.sf.gov/reports--january-2025--rent-board-referral-listing) (including **Open Door Legal's Sunset Office at 1722 Irving St, SF 94122**, Housing Rights Committee Richmond Office, SF Tenants Union, and Eviction Defense Collaborative, subject to eligibility/capacity). A notice survives scrutiny when it clears all **15 statutory gates**: active `§37.15` license, no fixed-term bar, verified base rent excluding passthroughs, exact non-compounded/non-rounded `Form 571` math, valid `§827`/`§1013` service + proof, zero unabated >35-day citations ([Civ. Code §1942.4](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CIV&sectionNum=1942.4)), all requested repairs completed before the effective date ([Topic 353 — 60-day deferral petition](https://www.sf.gov/information--failure-repair-and-maintain-petitions)), and clean `§1942.5` retaliation screening.
4. **Raising One Unit vs. Both Units ([`/compliance/fair-housing.html`](fair-housing.html)):**
   Raising **one unit and not the other is NOT automatically discrimination** if driven by objective, documented unit-specific facts (e.g., Unit 1 is inside an active fixed-term lease while Unit 2 is month-to-month). However, **when both units are legally eligible**, applying a **uniform, neutral formula to both units** (such as the routine `1.6%` annual CPI allowance on each unit's lawful date, or a pre-written objective formula tied strictly to lease/ledger records) is the strongest anti-discrimination posture under [42 U.S.C. §3604](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2023-title42/html/USCODE-2023-title42-chap45-subchapI-sec3604.htm), [Cal. Gov. Code §12955 (FEHA)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=GOV&sectionNum=12955), [Cal. Civ. Code §51 (Unruh)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CIV&sectionNum=51), and [SF Admin. Code §37.10B(a)(9)](https://www.sf.gov/information--sec-3710b-tenant-harassment). **Never** raise an ineligible unit merely to look symmetrical.
