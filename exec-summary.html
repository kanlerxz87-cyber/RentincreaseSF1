Executive Summary

What the law allows these two units to be charged, what is still unproven, and what to do about it.

Bottom line

These are two units in a 1940 building in the Outer Sunset. Under the San Francisco Rent Ordinance the owner may raise each tenant's rent once every twelve months by the Rent Board's published percentage — 1.6% for anything effective between 1 March 2026 and 28 February 2027 — applied to the tenant's base rent, plus any prior-year allowances that were skipped and have properly banked.

For Unit A that means $63.92 a month, taking $3,995.00 to $4,058.92. For Unit B the safe figure is $50.40 (to $3,200.40); a larger figure up to $3,562.65 is defensible only if the rent history proves that no increase has been taken since 1 June 2019, and only if the Rent Board's own banking-test conflict is resolved first. Anything above those figures requires a Rent Board petition, and “the market is higher” is expressly not a sufficient ground.

What must be confirmed before serving a notice

  1. Unit B's rent ledger. The 11.7%–13.1% ceiling assumes no increase since 1 June 2019. Without the notices and payment records, only 1.6% is safe (F04).
  2. Which banking test the Rent Board applies. Rule 4.12 says 12 months; the Rent Board's explanatory page says 24. The difference is 1.4 percentage points and $44.10 a month (F01).
  3. Unit A's tenancy status. If one original tenant never left, the added occupant cannot trigger any charge and the anniversary stays 1 February (F03).
  4. The certificate of occupancy. Coverage is inferred from 1940 and should be checked on the City's Property Information Map (F05).

How to speak about the increase to a tenant

The most defensible framing is also the simplest, and it is the one a tenant lawyer can check: the increase is the Rent Board's published annual allowable increase for the effective date, calculated on base rent, served with the dollar amount, the percentage and the effective date, once in twelve months, with the banked portion and its source years itemised where banking is used. No other justification is required for the annual amount, and none should be improvised — invented reasons create exposure that the correct notice does not S01 S08 S07.
