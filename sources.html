Source policy

Every factual claim on this site resolves to one of the 24 entries below. Nothing is cited from memory, and no value appears anywhere on the site without a source id next to it. Where an official source could not be found, the value is left blank and recorded on Flagged for Review rather than filled from a secondary source. Two secondary sources are used, and both are labelled as such: an SEC filing (a primary filing, but a company document rather than a regulator's) and the programme operator for the Tenant Right to Counsel programme.

S04Official — City & County of San FranciscoBanked rent increases
SF Rent Board (sf.gov)
Banking since 4/1/1982; no cap on banked amount; not compounded or prorated; does not expire during a tenancy; new owners may use predecessors' banked amounts; does not carry to a new tenant; states a 24-month test (see F01).
S05Official — City & County of San FranciscoAnnual rent increase for multiple years
SF Rent Board (sf.gov)
Multi-year increases are added, not compounded; no petition required; no rounding up; 30/35-day notice; worked 8.2% example.
S06Official — City & County of San FranciscoLearn about San Francisco rental laws
SF Rent Board (sf.gov)
Rent control + just cause for units built on or before 13 June 1979 that are not single-family dwellings; list of partial/full exemptions; Property Information Map for build date.
S07Official — City & County of San FranciscoRules 4.10 (Notice) and 4.12 (Banking)
SF Rent Board Rules and Regulations, Part IV
Verbatim text of Rule 4.12(a)/(b): 12-month banking test, one increase per 12 months, no compounding/rounding, disclosure of banked portion and dates; Rule 4.10(b): non-conforming increase is null and void.
S08Official — City & County of San FranciscoAdministrative Code Chapter 37 (Rent Ordinance) as amended 24 Nov 2024

§37.3(a)(1) annual increase ≤60% of CPI and ≤7%, license required; §37.3(a)(2) banking; §37.3(a)(8) above-allowable increases need a §37.8 petition; §37.3(a)(11) no increase for additional occupants; §37.3(b) notice content and void-if-nonconforming; §37.10C algorithmic-device ban.
S09Official — City & County of San FranciscoThe Rent Board Housing Inventory / rent increase license
SF Rent Board (sf.gov)
Annual reporting deadline 1 March; a rent increase license is required before any annual or banked increase can take effect; notice is not enforceable without a license on the effective date.
S10Official — City & County of San FranciscoLandlord Petitions and Passthroughs
SF Rent Board (sf.gov)
The six increase types that require a petition (capital improvement, O&M, special circumstances/comparable rents, Prop I rent history, utility passthrough, Rule 1.21); no filing fee; petitions must precede the notice.
S11Official — City & County of San FranciscoOperating and Maintenance Expense Petitions
SF Rent Board (sf.gov), topic 322
O&M petition allows up to 7% extra only if O&M expenses rose more than the annual allowable increase over two comparison years; must be filed before the notice; becomes part of base rent.
S12Official — City & County of San FranciscoSpecial Circumstances Petitions Based on Rents for Comparable Units
SF Rent Board (sf.gov), topic 323
"The mere fact that a long-standing tenant is paying significantly less than market rent is not considered an extraordinary circumstance"; petition required; one-time only; precludes other increases.
S13Official — City & County of San FranciscoTenant Petitions
SF Rent Board (sf.gov)
Free tenant petitions include Unlawful Rent Increase / determination of lawful rent and Tenant Summary Petition for an invalid notice; the Rent Board does not adjudicate harassment, discrimination or retaliation.
S14Official — State of CaliforniaCivil Code §1947.12 (Tenant Protection Act / AB 1482)
California Legislative Information
Statewide cap = 5% + CPI change, max 10%; max two increments per 12 months; new-tenancy reset; §1947.12(d)(3) exempts units under a stricter local ordinance.
S15Official — State of CaliforniaCivil Code §827 (notice of rent increase)
California Legislative Information
30 days' notice if the increase is 10% or less; 90 days if greater than 10%; service personally or by mail per Code of Civil Procedure §1013; longer period controls if another law requires it.
S16Official — State of CaliforniaCivil Code §1954.52 (Costa-Hawkins)
California Legislative Information
Owner may set initial and subsequent rates for units with a certificate of occupancy after 1 Feb 1995 or that are separately alienable — the exemptions relied on by newer condos/SFRs, not by this 1940 multi-unit building.
S17Official — State of CaliforniaCivil Code §1954.53 (vacancy re-pricing)
California Legislative Information
An owner may establish the initial rental rate for a new tenancy, except where the prior tenancy was terminated by the owner's notice or a §827 change in terms.
S18Official — State of CaliforniaCivil Code §1942.5 (retaliation)
California Legislative Information
A landlord may not increase rent or reduce services in retaliation for a tenant's repair complaint, agency complaint, inspection, or tenant-organising activity, including a 180-day window after specified tenant actions.
S19Official — State of CaliforniaGovernment Code §12955 (FEHA — housing discrimination)
California Legislative Information
Unlawful for an owner to discriminate or harass because of race, color, religion, sex, gender identity/expression, sexual orientation, marital status, national origin, ancestry, familial status, source of income, disability, veteran/military status or genetic information; §12955(f) bars retaliatory eviction/discrimination.
S20Official — FederalHousing Discrimination Under the Fair Housing Act
U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development
Federal Fair Housing Act prohibits discrimination in rental housing because of race, color, national origin, religion, sex, familial status or disability.
S21Official — City & County of San FranciscoRent Board Referral Listing (free and low-cost tenant resources)

S22Program operator (non-government)What is Tenant Right to Counsel — Prop F
Eviction Defense Collaborative
City-funded Tenant Right to Counsel programme: tenants who received an eviction notice or an unlawful detainer and do not live with the landlord have a right to a free attorney, full-scope representation. EDC is named as the intake point in the Rent Board's official §37.9(c) notice form.
S23Primary filing — SECForm 10-K for fiscal year ended 31 Dec 2024 (filed with the SEC)
AvalonBay Communities, Inc.
Institutional-owner practice: leases of one year or less; staggering lease expirations to seasonal demand; premium pricing for customer segments; centralised renewal activity; explicit statement that rents and renewal increases are limited by fair housing laws and by state/local rent regulations.

The City body that investigates and mediates discrimination complaints in housing, employment and public accommodations — the local forum for a discrimination claim (the Rent Board does not hear these).
