# Executive summary — San Francisco rent increases, Outer Sunset 94122

**Verified live:** 2026-11-11 (29 official sources re-fetched and read; see `sources.json` in this folder).
**Published dashboard:** [https://kanlerxz87-cyber.github.io/RentincreaseSF1/](https://kanlerxz87-cyber.github.io/RentincreaseSF1/) — landing page plus category subpages under `hub/`, assurance record under `assurance/`, Flagged for Review under `hub/flags.html`.
**Privacy:** Tenant identities and the street address are withheld. Tenants appear only as placeholders (Unit 1 — Tenants 1A and 1B; Unit 2 — Tenants 2A and 2B). Dates and dollar amounts supplied in the brief are retained unchanged as anonymized scenario inputs. This summary is research, not legal advice.

> **Bottom line:** Yes. An individual owner of this reported two-unit rent-controlled building must report each tenant-occupied unit through the Rent Board Housing Inventory and have a current Rent Increase License for each unit when an annual or banked increase takes effect. Incorporation is not required and there is no individual-owner exemption. The safer sequence is to finish reporting before serving notice; the statutory hard gate is licensure on the **effective date**. Ordinary annual/banked notices go to the tenant, not to the Board for per-increase approval.
>
> Sources: [SF Administrative Code §37.15](https://www.sf.gov/information/sec-3715-reporting-obligations-licensing) · [Rent Board Housing Inventory](https://www.sf.gov/rent-board-housing-inventory)

## 1. Forefront answer — do I need a licence and Rent Board notice as an individual owner?

**Yes, you need the licence. No, you do not need per-increase Board approval.** The two ideas are often merged into one question, so they are separated here.

| Question | Answer | Official source |
|---|---|---|
| Do I need a Rent Increase License as an individual (not a corporation)? | Yes. §37.15 applies to owners of units subject to Chapter 37 and contains no individual-owner or two-unit exemption. | [§37.15](https://www.sf.gov/information/sec-3715-reporting-obligations-licensing) |
| How is the licence obtained? | Report each unit in the Housing Inventory via the Rent Board Portal. After substantial compliance, the Board automatically generates the licence for each non-owner-occupied unit. Reporting is free. | [Housing Inventory](https://www.sf.gov/rent-board-housing-inventory) |
| Must I inform the Rent Board before notifying tenants? | Best practice: yes — complete reporting and confirm both licences **before** serving notice. Hard legal gate: a current licence must be on file on the **effective date** of the increase. | [Housing Inventory](https://www.sf.gov/rent-board-housing-inventory) · [§37.15(e)](https://www.sf.gov/information/sec-3715-reporting-obligations-licensing) |
| Must I file each increase with the Board for approval? | No. Ordinary annual/banked increases do not require a petition or approval. The written notice goes to the tenant. The licence does not need to be attached to the notice. | [Housing Inventory](https://www.sf.gov/rent-board-housing-inventory) · [Learn about rent increases](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-francisco) |
| What if I report late? | Late submission is allowed and no monetary penalty applies for late filing, but the increase is unenforceable without a licence on the effective date. Restoration is prospective; the tenant does not owe the increase for suspension months. | [§37.15(e)](https://www.sf.gov/information/sec-3715-reporting-obligations-licensing) · [Housing Inventory](https://www.sf.gov/rent-board-housing-inventory) |
| When is reporting due? | Annually by March 1 for buildings with fewer than 10 units (operative March 1, 2023). Update owner/manager contact information within 30 days of a change. | [§37.15(c)](https://www.sf.gov/information/sec-3715-reporting-obligations-licensing) |

It does not affect whether a rent increase is lawful. | [Treasurer lessor guidance](https://sftreasurer.org/information-lessors-residential-real-estate) · [Register a Business](https://sftreasurer.org/business/register-business) |

### Three City registrations that must not be confused

| Registration | Issued by | What it does | Effect on rent increase |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rent Increase License | SF Rent Board via Housing Inventory | Licences annual/banked increases under §37.3(a)(1)-(2) | **Gates** the increase: must be current on effective date. [§37.15](https://www.sf.gov/information/sec-3715-reporting-obligations-licensing) |
| Business Registration Certificate | SF Treasurer & Tax Collector | General business-tax registration | **No effect** on lawfulness of a rent increase. On the reported two units in one building, guidance points to no registration, conditionally. [Lessor guidance](https://sftreasurer.org/information-lessors-residential-real-estate) |
| Rent Board Fee | SF Rent Board | $59.00 per dwelling unit for 2026-27; due March 1; exemption request deadline December 14, 2026 for the 2027 fee | Separate from the free licence. Fee exemption does not satisfy Housing Inventory reporting. [Rent Board Fee](https://www.sf.gov/rent-board-fee) |

## 3. Corporate versus individual-owner practice

| Practice point | What official sources establish | Official source |
|---|---|---|
| Core duties are owner-neutral | The same Housing Inventory, licence, rate, banking, and notice duties apply regardless of corporate form. No official SF dataset measuring typical increase behavior by individual versus corporate owners was located; do not turn that absence into a stereotype. | [§37.15](https://www.sf.gov/information/sec-3715-reporting-obligations-licensing) · [Learn about rent increases](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-francisco) |
| Algorithmic pricing tools | San Francisco prohibits selling or using an algorithmic device (revenue-management software using nonpublic competitor data to advise on rent/occupancy). Aggregated public-data reports that do not recommend rents are excluded. General property-management software is not banned merely because it is software. | [§37.10C](https://www.sf.gov/information--sec-3710c-use-and-sale-algorithmic-devices-prohibited) |
| Federal enforcement example (bounded) | DOJ's November 24, 2025 RealPage announcement describes **allegations** about shared nonpublic competitor information and a **proposed** settlement. It is not a final adjudication and does not establish typical conduct by every corporate landlord. | [DOJ announcement](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/justice-department-requires-realpage-end-sharing-competitively-sensitive-information-and) |
| Per-unit controls (either owner type) | Current reporting/licence evidence, actual lease and ledger, reproducible calculation, neutral selection, timely notice, and proof of service. | [§37.15](https://www.sf.gov/information/sec-3715-reporting-obligations-licensing) · [Rules 4.10/4.12](https://www.sf.gov/reports--december-2022--rent-board-rules-and-regulations-part-iv-rent-increases-not-requiring) |

## 6. Raise one unit or both?

| Proposition | Answer | Official source |
|---|---|---|
| Raising one unit and not the other is automatically discrimination | No. Different treatment is not automatically discrimination. Selecting a unit because of a protected characteristic, retaliating, or acting in bad faith can be unlawful. | [FEHA §12955](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=GOV&sectionNum=12955) · [HUD overview](https://www.hud.gov/helping-americans/fair-housing-act-overview) · [§37.10B](https://www.sf.gov/information--sec-3710b-tenant-harassment) |
| Raising both identically is a safe harbour | No. Identical treatment is not immunity, and an ineligible unit should not be raised merely for symmetry. | [FEHA §12955](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=GOV&sectionNum=12955) |
| Recommended method | Use a neutral, record-based method for both units: actual lease, verified base rent, last increase, published rate, and proven banked components. Differences should follow those records, not assumptions or protected traits. | [§37.15](https://www.sf.gov/information/sec-3715-reporting-obligations-licensing) · [Form 571](https://www.sf.gov/documents/34344/571_Allowable_Annual_Increases_26-27_1.pdf) |
| Retaliation timing | Civil Code §1942.5 is not an automatic 180-day moratorium on every increase. It addresses retaliation under stated conditions, preserves lawful cause, and contains a good-faith-ground provision. Screen the history rather than claiming either automatic illegality or automatic clearance. | [Civil Code §1942.5](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CIV&sectionNum=1942.5) |
