| I-1 | **Banking look-back conflict.** The sf.gov banking page says a skipped increase is banked only if "at least 24 full months pass without any rent increase," and that otherwise "the skipped increase is lost." Rules § 4.12(a) says "a full 12 months must have elapsed" from the date the increase could have been imposed. The page does not say where the 24 months start.
