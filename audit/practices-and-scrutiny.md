## Key Official-Source Conclusions

1. **Corporate REITs vs. Individual SF Property Owners (`dashboard/data/practices.json`):**
   - **Empirical Structure (`2024 HUD/Census RHFS`):** Small `1-unit` (`82.9%`) and `2–4 unit` (`14.6%`) properties account for `97.5%` of U.S. rental properties (`$5,820/unit` mean operating expenses; median DSCR `74`), whereas `50+ unit` corporate properties represent `0.4%` of properties / `39.2%` of units (`$10,979/unit` mean operating expenses; median DSCR `164`).
   - **SF Regulatory Convergence & Divergence:** Both corporate and individual owners of covered 1940 SF two-unit properties are bound by the same `1.6%` annual CPI cap (`§37.3`) and annual `March 1` Housing Inventory licensing (`§37.15`). However, institutional operators' use of algorithmic pricing software is now **prohibited in SF (`§37.10C`, up to `$1,000/unit/month` penalty + fees)** and prosecuted under federal/state antitrust law (*U.S. & California v. RealPage*), and post-2018 acquisition debt/tax reassessment cannot be passed through via O&M petitions (`Rule 6.10`).

2. **Statutory Notification & Lawful Reasons (`dashboard/data/notice_protocol.json`):**
   - Written notice only (`Cal. Civ. Code §827`), served personally (`30 days` if `≤10%`; `90 days` if `>10%`) or by first-class U.S. Mail within California (`+5 calendar days` = `35` or `95` days per `CCP §1013(a)`), never by email/text alone.
   - Itemize base rent, current annual `%` and `$`, banked `%` and `$` with exact prior anniversary dates (`Rule 4.12(b)`), unrounded new base rent, and active `§37.15` license status; bill any `$29.50` (`50%` of `$59.00`) Rent Board Fee separately from base rent.

Every potential defect—unlicensed `§37.15` status, fixed-term lease bar on Unit 1, premature `<24-month` banking on Unit 1, rounding up, mail-service day count, `>35-day` DBI citation (`CC §1942.4`), `180-day` repair retaliation (`CC §1942.5`), and `§37.10B` constructive-eviction shock—is neutralized by the recommended protocol.

4. **One Unit vs. Both Units (`dashboard/data/discrimination.json`):**
   - California FEHA (`Gov. Code §12955`), Unruh (`Civ. Code §51`), and SF `§37.10B(a)(9)` have **no multi-unit "Mrs. Murphy" exemption** for a 2-unit rental building.
   - Adopting a documented, uniform building-wide policy applying **`1.6%` annual CPI** to **both Unit 1 and Unit 2** on each unit's earliest lawful month-to-month eligibility date eliminates comparator-household disparate-treatment claims while avoiding a `13.1%` shock on Unit 2 or a roommate-targeted hike on Unit 1.
