5. **Misstatement of Rule 4.10(b) / Rule 4.12(b) Null-and-Void Effect in `README.md` (`IRREGULARITY RA-04`):**
   - `README.md` states: *"Two administrative prerequisites can void an otherwise correct increase: a current rent-increase licence (§37.15) and a notice that itemises the annual and banked portions (§37.3(b), Rule 4.10)."*
   - Direct verification against enacted **SF Rent Board Rules & Regulations Section 4.10(b) and Section 4.12(b)** (`https://www.sf.gov/reports/december-2022/rent-board-rules-and-regulations-part-iv-rent-increases-not-requiring`) shows the opposite rule when total rent demanded does not exceed the lawful annual + banked limit: Rule `4.10(b)` exempts increases where *"the amount requested equals no more than the allowable annual and banked rent increase(s)"*, and Rule `4.12(b)` explicitly states: *"provided, however, that failure to include such information shall not render the increase null and void."* (Itemization is still mandatory best practice to pass SF tenant-lawyer review without a Rent Board dispute, but omission does not void an otherwise within-cap annual/banked notice.)
6. **Mailed Notice Service Cutoff Error in `README.md` & `data/units.csv` (`IRREGULARITY RA-05`):**
   - `README.md` states: *"Requires 90 days' notice, so the last practical service date for an effective date of 28 Feb 2027 is 30 Nov 2026."*
   - Under `Cal. Civ. Code § 827(b)(1)(B)` and `Cal. Code Civ. Proc. § 1013(a)`, service by mail within California extends the notice period by **5 calendar days** (`90 + 5 = 95 days`). Counting backward 95 calendar days from `2027-02-28`, the last date to mail a >10% increase effective `2027-02-28` is **November 25, 2026**, not November 30, 2026 (and even for personal delivery, 90 full days prior to `2027-02-28` is **November 30, 2026**).
7. **Unit 1 Placeholder Tenant Alias Ambiguity in `data/units.csv` (`IRREGULARITY RA-06`):**
   - `data/units.csv` lists Unit 1's current household as `"Tenant 1A and Tenant 1B"`. Because Unit 1 began on February 1, 2025 with **three** original co-tenants (`1A, 1B, 1C`) and transitioned on April 1, 2026 to **one continuing original tenant (`1A`) plus one new replacement co-tenant (`1D`)**, calling the current pair `"1A and 1B"` misidentifies the new occupant as an original February 2025 co-tenant—obscuring the critical `Cal. Civ. Code § 1954.53(d)` / SF Rent Board `Rule 6.14` distinction between original occupants and subsequent co-tenants/subtenants.
8. **Unverified Fixed-Term Lease Assumption on Unit 1 (`IRREGULARITY RA-07`):**
   - `data/units.csv` recommends `2027-02-01` as Unit 1's next lawful increase date (`1.6%` or `3.0%` with banking). However, Unit 1 executed a continuing-tenancy arrangement on `April 1, 2026` at `$3,995/month`. Whether that April 1, 2026 instrument is a month-to-month addendum or a new 12-month fixed-term lease (through `March 31, 2027`) cannot be verified from official sources; under `Cal. Civ. Code § 827(a)`, unilateral rent increases cannot take effect during an active fixed-term lease unless the lease expressly permits mid-term adjustments.
9. **Official SF.gov Worked-Example Date Typos (`IRREGULARITY RA-08`):**
