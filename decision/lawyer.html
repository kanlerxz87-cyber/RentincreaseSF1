Current status of those 16 gates on this file

Gates 1, 2, 4, 7, 9, 13 and 14 cannot be answered from the brief alone — they depend on licence records, ledgers and private facts that were not supplied. Gates 5, 6, 8, 10, 11 and 12 are rules rather than facts and can be satisfied by drafting correctly. Gate 16 is a live risk on Unit B. Gate 3 depends on the reporting you now file. Gate 15 is not triggered on the reported facts because an original occupant remains in Unit A.

Honest summary: on the information available today, this file would not survive an unqualified “done reasonably, normal business practice, no discrimination” sign-off — not because anything was done wrong, but because the licence status and the ledgers are unknown. That is fixable in an afternoon of document retrieval, and the open questions page sets out exactly which documents to pull.

Free, officially verified routes a tenant can use

  • Rent Board counselling, free, 415-252-4600, weekdays 9am-12pm and 1pm-4pm — available to landlords as well as tenants.
  • Tenant Summary Petition (Form 518) if the increase has not yet taken effect and there is no licence.
  • Tenant Petition for Unlawful Rent Increase (Form 516C) once it has taken effect; may be set for a hearing before an Administrative Law Judge and can lead to a rent refund.
  • Portal licence lookup by address or Assessor's Parcel Number, including previous years.

San Francisco's Tenant Right to Counsel programme was not confirmed on an official sf.gov page during this pass — only commercial summaries were found, so it is flagged rather than asserted (F-19).
