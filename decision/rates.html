Before you act on any banked figure

File No. 261020 is pending before the Land Use and Transportation Committee. As drafted it would cap combined banked and capital-improvement increases at 10% per year for three years, and would apply to banked increases noticed on or after 15 September 2026. It is not law — Legistar showed “Pending Committee Action” on 2026-11-13 — but the Rent Board has posted a formal warning about it on its own rate pages. Unit B's illustrative banked portion is 13.1%, which is above the proposed cap. See F-10.
