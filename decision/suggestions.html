Suggestions for review

Nothing here has been implemented — pick a path first

Each suggestion below is written so it can be accepted, deferred or rejected on its own. Every one states what it costs, what it reduces, and which official source makes it worth doing. No change has been made to the property, the tenancies or the repository's existing canonical site.

How to read this page

Each row is one decision. Priority reflects how much legal exposure it removes per unit of effort. Effort is the owner's time, not a fee estimate. Risk if skipped is what remains unresolved. Choose with the open questions page beside you: several suggestions exist purely to close a question.

S-01 Confirm and, if needed, complete the Housing Inventory for both units before serving anything. The licence is the gate. An increase effective while unlicensed is null and void, and the tenant can look this up in the portal in minutes. Low — one portal session per unit, free High. The entire increase can be void, potentially for multiple years Accept / defer to a fixed date / reject with written reasoning S02 S09 S08
S-02 Screenshot and file the licence record for each unit, dated, for every relevant year. The Board advises checking “multiple years for all relevant dates”. A dated screenshot is the cheapest evidence you will ever create. Low Medium — you cannot later prove the licence existed on the effective date Accept / defer / reject S02 S08
S-03 Rebuild both rent ledgers from the tenancy start, month by month. Banking entitlement is entirely a ledger question. The 24-month rule means one missed or mis-dated entry changes the number. Medium High. Every banked calculation is unsupported without it Accept / defer / reject S04 S05
S-04 Resolve Unit A's anniversary date before calculating anything. The 2026-04-01 occupant change may have moved the anniversary from 1 February to 1 April, which changes both the applicable rate and the notice deadline. Low — read the agreement and the ledger Medium — a notice served against the wrong anniversary is defective Accept / defer / reject S03
S-05 Confirm official coverage for both units (parcel build date and any exemption) rather than relying on the reported 1940 date. Rent control attaches to units built on or before 13 June 1979, subject to exemptions. Everything downstream depends on it. Low — Property Information Map, plus a Rent Board counselling call if unclear Medium — the entire analysis could be applied to the wrong regime Accept / defer / reject S11 S13
S-06 Do not serve any large banked catch-up until File No. 261020 is resolved. As drafted, the 10% cap would reach banked increases noticed on or after 15 September 2026 — i.e. notices served from today onward. Low — a calendar reminder and a Legistar check High for Unit B. Its banked portion alone is above the proposed cap Accept / defer to a decision date / reject with advice S20 S04
S-07 Decide one unit or both on the ledgers, and write the reason down before serving. Uniformity is not a defence and is not required. What protects you is a neutral, dated, ledger-based reason. Low Medium — the reason would otherwise be reconstructed after the fact Accept / defer / reject S19 S18 S03
S-08 Use a notice template that shows the arithmetic and itemises the banked years. The Rent Board lists “which part of the increase is banked” as a required element; it is the one most often missing. Low Medium — a defect that invites a petition even where the number is right Accept / defer / reject S04 S14
S-09 Add 5 days if you mail the notice, and keep proof of service. Civ. Code §827(b) expressly makes mailed service subject to CCP §1013. Low Medium — a short notice is a void notice Accept / defer / reject S14
S-10 Check the 180-day retaliation window for both households before serving. A tenantability complaint, agency complaint, inspection or citation inside 180 days blocks the increase. Low Medium — timing, not amount, becomes the defect Accept / defer / reject S16
S-11 Pay the Rent Board Fee and the Housing Inventory as two separate tasks. The Board states expressly that a fee exemption does not satisfy reporting, and reporting does not exempt the fee. Low Low — but a missed fee accrues penalties, and a missed report voids increases Accept / defer / reject S12 S02
S-12 Decide the privacy posture before filing the Housing Inventory. The submission is public and can carry owner/manager name and business contact, plus unit characteristics. Rent is reported in $250 bands, not exact dollars. Low Medium — an avoidable public disclosure Accept / defer / reject S02 S01
S-13 Set one annual calendar entry: 1 March. The Housing Inventory update and the Rent Board Fee fall on the same date each year; the fee exemption window closes in December. Low Medium — the licence lapses silently Accept / defer / reject S02 S12
S-14 Have a Rent Board counsellor sanity-check the calculation before serving. Free counselling is available to landlords as well as tenants on 415-252-4600. Low Medium — a free check is being left unused Accept / defer / reject S11
S-15 Consider whether the annual-only route is the better first move. Taking the current published percentage, without touching banked amounts, keeps both units far below the 10% threshold and the proposed cap, and preserves banking for later. Low Low — the cost is foregone income, not legal exposure Accept / defer / reject S03 S04 S20
S-16 Site: collapse the parallel editions or mark them clearly superseded. More than a dozen editions now exist; several predate the current rate and the File No. 261020 warning. Medium — a repository task Low legal, medium practical — contradicts nothing, but confuses the record Accept / defer / reject S07 S20

What has deliberately NOT been suggested

  • No suggested dollar figure has been recommended as “the” increase. The scenarios are arithmetic on the brief's figures; the choice is the owner's.
  • No template notice has been drafted as a finished legal document. The content requirements are listed on the notice page; the drafting should follow the resolved ledger.
  • No change has been made to the repository's existing canonical site. The root index.html is generated by hub/build.py and enforced by the “Final assurance” workflow, and that code is owned by completed sessions. This edition is additive and sits at /decision/. See F-14.
  • No legal conclusion has been reached about whether any proposed increase would be upheld. That depends on facts this site does not have.
