Why the two units land so far apart

Unit 1's tenancy began in February 2025. Only the 1.4% year can have been skipped, and only if the 24-month test is met by February 2027, giving 3.0% at most. Unit 2's tenancy began in June 2019. Six consecutive years were skipped, and every one of them is banked because each is more than 24 months in the past, giving 11.5% banked plus the current 1.6% — 13.1%.

Note the two 1992–93 entries. Only one of them may be imposed; the Rent Board flags that on the same document. It does not affect either unit here, because both tenancies post-date it, but it is the kind of detail that makes a hand-built calculation go wrong.
