Two wording traps

Rule 4.10 versus Rule 4.12. Rule 4.10(b) says a non-conforming notice makes the whole increase void, but it carves out an increase that equals no more than the allowable annual and banked amounts. Rule 4.12(b) says omitting the banked breakdown "shall not render the increase null and void". A notice that states the correct total but omits the banked breakdown is therefore very likely enforceable — but it hands a tenant-side reviewer the first thing to argue about, for no gain. State the breakdown.

The percentage must match the effective date. A notice served in November 2026 for a February 2027 effective date uses the rate in force in February 2027 (1.6%), not whatever the Board announces next. If the increase is pushed past 28 February 2027 the whole calculation has to be redone.
