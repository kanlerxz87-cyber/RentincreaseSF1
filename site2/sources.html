S04SF.gov (Rent Board) — Banked rent increasesBanking concept; add never compound; no limit; banked increases do not expire in a tenancy; not carried over to a new tenant; notice must say which part is banked. NOTE: this page states a 24-month test which conflicts with Rule 4.12 and with the Board's own worked examples — see F01.“A skipped increase is only banked if at least 24 full months pass without any rent increase. If less than 24 months pass, the skipped increase is lost and cannot be imposed at a later date.”
S05SF.gov (Rent Board) — Rent Board Rules and Regulations: Part IV, Section 4.12 BankingBinding text of the banking rule: 12 months, not 24; only increases available on/after 4/1/1982; not compounded; not rounded up; notice of the banked portion and the dates it is based on; omission of that information does not void the increase“A full 12 months must have elapsed from the date that an annual rent increase, or a portion thereof, could have been imposed before this banking section becomes applicable.”

S07SF.gov (Rent Board) — Rules and Regulations: Part XIII, Section 13.14 Licensing – Notice of Rent IncreaseAn increase that takes effect while the owner is unlicensed is null and void in its entirety; the effective date (not the service date) is the test; applies to buildings with fewer than 10 units for increases effective on or after 3/1/2023“Any rent increase under Ordinance Sections 37.3(a)(1)-(2) purporting to be effective while the landlord is unlicensed shall render the entire rent increase null and void; provided, however, that the effective date of the rent increase, not the date of service, shall be used to determine whether or not the increase conforms with the provisions of this section.”
