How the percentage is set

On 1 March each year the Rent Board publishes the increase in the CPI for the preceding twelve months and grants landlords a licence to impose an annual increase of up to 60% of that figure — never more than 7% S08. The published figure is applied to the tenant's base rent, which includes housing services such as parking or storage but excludes temporary passthroughs and fluctuating charges S01. Increases may not be rounded up to the nearest dollar, and may not be compounded S01 S04.
