## Legal Framework

### 1. Local Law (San Francisco Rent Ordinance - Administrative Code Chapter 37)
- **Coverage:** Most units in buildings built before June 13, 1979 [1](https://cityrulelookup.com/rental-property/rent-control/san-francisco-ca) [4](https://urbantaken.com/understanding-rent-control-in-san-francisco/)
- **Building Built 1940:** Qualifies for rent control coverage [1](https://cityrulelookup.com/rental-property/rent-control/san-francisco-ca)
- **Allowable Annual Increase (March 1, 2025 – February 28, 2026):** 1.4% [1](https://cityrulelookup.com/rental-property/rent-control/san-francisco-ca) [2](https://landlordos.net/learn/questions/sf-annual-rent-increase-2026)
- **Allowable Annual Increase (March 1, 2026 – February 28, 2027):** 1.6% [1](https://www.sf.gov/reports--current-rates-including-rent-increase-relocation-sec-deposit) [2](https://www.bancalsf.com/banked-rent-increases-san-francisco)
- **Calculation Formula:** 60% of Bay Area CPI, capped at 7% maximum [2](https://landlordos.net/learn/questions/sf-annual-rent-increase-2026)
- **Banking Provision:** Unused allowable increases can be accumulated and imposed later (Section 37.3(a)(1) and Rules & Regulations section 4.12) [2](https://www.bancalsf.com/banked-rent-increases-san-francisco)
- **Notice Requirements:**
  - 30 days written notice for increases under 10% [2](https://landlordos.net/learn/questions/sf-annual-rent-increase-2026) [4](https://www.lifetimepm.com/blog/california-rent-increase-guide-2026)
  - 90 days written notice for increases over 10% [2](https://landlordos.net/learn/questions/sf-annual-rent-increase-2026) [4](https://www.lifetimepm.com/blog/california-rent-increase-guide-2026)
- **Rent Board Fee:** $59 per dwelling unit (due March 1) [3](https://www.bancalsf.com/sf-rent-board-housing-inventory)
- **Housing Inventory Reporting:** Required annually by March 1 [3](https://www.bancalsf.com/sf-rent-board-housing-inventory)

### 2. State Law (California Tenant Protection Act - AB 1482)
- **Cap:** 5% + regional CPI, maximum 10% in any 12-month period [1](https://calandlordtenantlaws.com/rent-control-ab-1482) [3](https://www.lifetimepm.com/blog/california-rent-increase-guide-2026)
- **Applicability:** SF Rent Ordinance (local) overrides AB 1482 for covered units (pre-1979 buildings) [4](https://www.lifetimepm.com/blog/california-rent-increase-guide-2026) [5](https://www.leaserunner.com/blog/month-to-month-rent-increase-california)
- **Notice:** 30 days (≤10%), 90 days (>10%) [3](https://www.lifetimepm.com/blog/california-rent-increase-guide-2026)
- **Expiration:** Scheduled to expire January 1, 2030 [3](https://www.lifetimepm.com/blog/california-rent-increase-guide-2026)

### 3. Federal Law
- **No federal cap** on private market rent increases [1](https://www.fflw.org/blog/how-much-can-a-landlord-raise-rent) [5](https://www.lethub.co/blog/how-much-can-you-raise-rent)
- **Fair Housing Act:** Prohibits discrimination based on protected classes (race, color, national origin, religion, sex, familial status, disability) [1](https://www.fflw.org/blog/how-much-can-a-landlord-raise-rent)
- **HUD-assisted housing:** Subject to program rules (not applicable here) [1](https://www.fflw.org/blog/how-much-can-a-landlord-raise-rent)
