## Legal Framework (Discrimination)

### Federal Law (Fair Housing Act)
- **Prohibition:** No discrimination based on race, color, national origin, religion, sex, familial status, or disability [1](https://www.fflw.org/blog/how-much-can-a-landlord-raise-rent)
- **Application:** Applies to rent increases - differential treatment could be discriminatory [1](https://www.fflw.org/blog/how-much-can-a-landlord-raise-rent)
- **Scrutiny:** SF tenant lawyers would review any differential treatment [1](https://www.fflw.org/blog/how-much-can-a-landlord-raise-rent)

### State Law (California)
- **AB 1482:** No specific discrimination provisions beyond Fair Housing Act [1](https://calandlordtenantlaws.com/rent-control-ab-1482) [3](https://www.lifetimepm.com/blog/california-rent-increase-guide-2026)
- **Just Cause Eviction:** Protections apply equally to all tenants [4](https://urbantaken.com/understanding-rent-control-in-san-francisco/)

### Local Law (San Francisco Rent Ordinance)
- **Equal Application:** Rent Board rates (1.4% for 2025-26, 1.6% for 2026-27) apply equally to all covered units [1](https://cityrulelookup.com/rental-property/rent-control/san-francisco-ca) [2](https://www.sf.gov/reports--current-rates-including-rent-increase-relocation-sec-deposit)
- **Notice Requirements:** Same for all units (30/90 days) [2](https://landlordos.net/learn/questions/sf-annual-rent-increase-2026) [4](https://www.lifetimepm.com/blog/california-rent-increase-guide-2026)

## Recommendation: Raise Both Units

### Rationale
- **Avoid Discrimination Claims:** Raising only one unit could be perceived as discriminatory under Fair Housing Act [1](https://www.fflw.org/blog/how-much-can-a-landlord-raise-rent)
- **Consistency:** Applying the same percentage increase (1.6%) to both units demonstrates fair business practice [1](https://www.fflw.org/blog/how-much-can-a-landlord-raise-rent)
- **Legal Scrutiny:** SF tenant lawyers would scrutinize any differential treatment [1](https://www.fflw.org/blog/how-much-can-a-landlord-raise-rent)
- **Business Practice:** Standard practice for big real estate corporations [2](https://www.bancalsf.com/banked-rent-increases-san-francisco) [3](https://christopherleesf.com/san-francisco-rent-control-laws-2025/)
