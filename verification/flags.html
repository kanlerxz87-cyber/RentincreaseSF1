F-01MediumOfficial wording on the licence mixes “effective date of the notice” with the effective date of the increase
The Housing Inventory page says a notice “will not be enforceable without having a rent increase license on the effective date of the notice”, while the same page says a licence must be on file “before any annual or banked rent increase can go into effect”. §37.15(e) ties the consequence to the unlicensed period.
The safe reading is to have the licence in place for the increase's effective date, and to confirm it before serving notice. This record states both rather than picking one.Have the licence current before notice and still current on the effective date. Confirm with a Rent Board counsellor if a gap exists.S03 S01
