The answer, in the order it matters

Yes — one individual owner still needs a Rent Increase Licence for each tenant-occupied unit. But there is no application about the increase itself.

The licence is not a form you send the Board about a specific increase. It is created automatically when you report the unit in the annual Housing Inventory, it is free, and it must be current when the increase takes effect. Ordinary annual and banked increases are not submitted to the Board for approval.

The one deadline that matters: a current licence on the increase's effective date. Without it the increase cannot take effect and the tenant does not owe it for the months the licence was suspended. Confirm the licence before you serve notice, so a fixable omission never becomes an unenforceable increase.
