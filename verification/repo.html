How the checks were run

The arithmetic was recomputed independently with exact decimal arithmetic rather than floating point, so a fraction of a cent cannot hide in a rounded display. The six notice dates were recomputed by subtracting the statutory period from the effective date. The published generator was re-run and produced no diff, which shows the committed pages match their data. All seven existing verification suites were executed on this tree and all pass.

Independent means independent. This record re-fetched the sources itself and formed its own view of each claim. Where it agreed with the published record it says so; where it found a narrower correct statement, it records the qualification; and where it found a defect it writes the defect down instead of editing around it.

Two things that should be fixed in the repository

Tracked cache files. Two compiled Python files are committed and there is no root .gitignore. They change on every local run, which manufactures diffs for the next session that works here. F-12

Two stale pull requests. Two earlier parallel editions were superseded by the integrated site but their pull requests are still open. They are harmless to the published site and are flagged rather than closed here. F-11

Privacy. No tenant names, initials, street address or private contact details are published here. The supplied dates and dollar amounts are retained unchanged, as instructed.

Scope. Every legal statement and every percentage on this site is quoted from an official source and linked in the row that uses it. Figures that could not be confirmed from an official source are left blank and listed on Flagged for review. Tenant-side figures use placeholders.
