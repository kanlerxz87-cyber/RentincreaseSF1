Why the amounts stay but the names do not

The figures are the question. Whether $63.92 on $3,995.00 is the right calculation cannot be checked without the numbers, so 1 February 2025, 1 April 2026, $3,995.00, 1 June 2019 and $3,150.00 are reproduced unchanged. They identify a rent, not a person.

The identities are not needed. Nothing in the analysis depends on who the tenants are. They appear as “two placeholders” and “one original tenant and one replacement occupant”, which preserves exactly the fact that matters — how many original occupants remained. S11

The street address is likewise withheld. The record refers only to the Outer Sunset and ZIP 94122, which is the level at which the Rent Ordinance and the published rate apply.

What a reader can and cannot infer

From this record a reader can learn the applicable rate, the method, the notice periods and the two units' rent history. They cannot identify the tenants, the building or the owner.
