S01Sec. 37.15 — Reporting Obligations; Licensing
SF Rent Board (sf.gov) — enacted ordinance
Local“(a) Owners of residential dwelling units subject to this Chapter 37 shall be required to report certain information about their units to the Rent Board … (e) If a landlord has substantially complied with the obligation to report information about a rental unit as required under this Section 37.15, the landlord shall receive a license to impose rent increases on tenants in that unit under Sections 37.3(a)(1)-(2). If the landlord has not substantially complied with the reporting obligation, then the license to impose rent increases shall be temporarily suspended during the period of the landlord's noncompliance. Upon receipt of the required information from the landlord, the suspension shall be terminated, and the license to impose rent increases shall be restored prospectively, but a tenant shall not be obligated to pay the increased rent for months during the period of suspension.”The licence, who must hold it, how it is obtained, and the consequence of not holding it.2026-11-08
S02Sec. 37.3 — Rent Limitations
SF Rent Board (sf.gov) — enacted ordinance
Local“(a)(1) … A landlord who has reported the required information about their rental unit to the Rent Board as set forth in Section 37.15 shall have a license to impose annually a rent increase which does not exceed a tenant's base rent by more than 60% of said published increase. In no event, however, shall the allowable annual increase be greater than 7%. … (b)(5) Nonconforming Rent Increases. Any rent increase which does not conform with the provisions of this section shall be null and void. … (a)(11)(A) … a landlord may not impose increases solely because a tenant has added an additional occupant to an existing tenancy … notwithstanding a rental agreement or lease that specifically permits a rent increase for additional occupants.”Annual allowance, 7% ceiling, licence condition in the ordinance, notice contents, null-and-void rule, additional-occupant prohibition.2026-11-08
S03The Rent Board Housing Inventory
SF Rent Board (sf.gov) — agency guidance
Local“Property owners who report that a unit is tenant-occupied will receive a rent increase license that allows them to impose annual allowable and banked rent increases. … Deadline: March 1 of every year. You can still make a Housing Inventory submission and receive a rent increase license after the deadline. However, your rent increase notice will not be enforceable without having a rent increase license on the effective date of the notice. … A landlord must have a current rent increase license on file with the Rent Board before any annual or banked rent increase can go into effect. The rent increase license does not need to be included with your rent increase notice. … Reporting is free. … The Rent Board does not assess monetary penalties for filing after the deadline.”Individual-owner duty, automatic licence generation, late reporting, annual deadline, effective-date gate, no attachment to notice, no late penalty.2026-11-08
S04Banked rent increases
SF Rent Board (sf.gov) — agency guidance
Local“A skipped increase is only banked if at least 24 full months pass without any rent increase. … Banked increases do not have to be approved by the Rent Board, but must be calculated correctly. S05Learn about rent increases in San Francisco
