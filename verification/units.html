Unit 1: why the lease matters more than the percentage

The reported facts are a lease beginning 1 February 2025 at $3,995.00, with a continuing tenancy attributed to 1 April 2026 at the same $3,995.00, and one original tenant still in place. Two consequences follow, and only one of them is arithmetic.

Three separate questions; a correct percentage does not answer the first two.

Two checks decide whether any of that is available.
