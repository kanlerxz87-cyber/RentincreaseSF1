## Unit 1

### Coverage and base rent

The reported 1940 construction year is consistent with an older building but does not prove coverage. Verify the unit's status, exemptions, regulatory agreements, and Rent Board records. The reported $3,995 continuation is not an authenticated current lawful base rent. Base rent excludes the ordinance's specified temporary passthrough categories. [SF coverage](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-san-francisco-rental-laws); [SF § 37.3](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/san_francisco/latest/sf_admin/0-0-0-70881); [increase guidance](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-francisco).

### Occupancy change

If Resident A was an original occupant under the rental agreement with the owner and remained in lawful possession, the reported partial change does not itself establish a Costa-Hawkins reset under § 37.3(d)(2)(B). Section 37.3(a)(11) also generally prohibits an increase solely because an occupant was added, subject to specified exceptions. The actual agreement, possession facts, notices, and any petition must be reviewed. Do not infer that a new document titled “lease” created a new tenancy. [SF § 37.3](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/san_francisco/latest/sf_admin/0-0-0-70881); [SF increase guidance](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-francisco).
