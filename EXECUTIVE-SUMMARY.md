## 1. Forefront: Rent Board Process, License, and Notification (Individual Owner)

**Question:** As an individual property owner (not a corporation), do I still need to apply for the license and inform the Rent Board before notifying tenants of a rent increase in San Francisco (Outer Sunset 94122)?

**Answer:** **YES.** Under San Francisco Rent Ordinance §37.15 and SF Rent Board rules, the requirement applies to **all** owners of residential property, including individual owners and non-corporations.

### Step-by-Step Process (Official Verified Links)

1. **Report to the Housing Inventory (Mandatory)**
   - **Action:** All owners of residential property must report into the Housing Inventory online through the Rent Board Portal. This applies even if you are an individual owner and not a corporation [4](https://www.sf.gov/rent-board-housing-inventory).
   - **Deadline:** March 1 of every year (reporting for 2026 was due March 1, 2026; late submissions are possible but the license will not be active until completed) [4](https://www.sf.gov/rent-board-housing-inventory).
   - **Link:** [SF Rent Board Housing Inventory](https://www.sf.gov/rent-board-housing-inventory) | [Portal](https://portal.sfrb.org/)

2. **Receive the Rent Increase License (Automatic Generation)**
   - **Action:** Once the Housing Inventory report is submitted for a tenant-occupied unit, the Rent Board **automatically generates** a Rent Increase License [4](https://www.sf.gov/rent-board-housing-inventory). There is no separate "application" form for the license itself; the inventory report *is* the trigger [2](https://www.bancalsf.com/sf-rent-board-housing-inventory).
   - **Requirement:** The license **must be in effect** on the date the increase takes effect. An increase imposed while the owner is unlicensed is null and void [2](https://www.bancalsf.com/sf-rent-board-housing-inventory) [4](https://www.sf.gov/rent-board-housing-inventory).
   - **Link:** [Learn about rent increases - Get a license](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-francisco/)

3. **Inform the Rent Board (Reporting)**
   - **Action:** The reporting into the Housing Inventory *is* the informing of the Rent Board. You must provide owner/manager contact info, unit details (square footage, bedrooms, bathrooms), occupancy dates, and base rent [4](https://www.sf.gov/rent-board-housing-inventory).
   - **Link:** [SF Rent Board Housing Inventory](https://www.sf.gov/rent-board-housing-inventory)

4. **Notify Tenants (After License is Active)**
   - **Action:** Only **after** the license is confirmed active (via the Portal or Inventory submission) should you serve the written notice to tenants.
   - **Timing:**
     - 30-day written notice for increases ≤ 10% [6](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-francisco/).
     - 90-day written notice for increases > 10% [6](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-francisco/).
     - Add 5 days if mailed [6](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-francisco/).
   - **Content:** Must include dollar amount, percentage, effective date, and breakdown of annual vs. banked portions [6](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-francisco/).
   - **Link:** [Learn about rent increases - Giving notice](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-francisco/)

**Conclusion:** The license and reporting **must** precede the tenant notification to ensure enforceability. Failure results in the increase being null and void [2](https://www.bancalsf.com/sf-rent-board-housing-inventory) [4](https://www.sf.gov/rent-board-housing-inventory).

## 3. Reasonable and Normal Rental Increases (SF 94122, Local/State/Federal)

### Local (SF Rent Ordinance) — Controlling [6](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-francisco/)
- **Annual Allowable Increase (2026-03-01 to 2027-02-28):** **1.6%** [1](https://www.sf.gov/departments--rent-board) [6](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-francisco/).
- **Calculation:** Base rent × 0.016 (no rounding up) [6](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-francisco/).
- **Banked Increases:** Allowed if ≥ 24 months pass without increase. Summed (not compounded) [6](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-francisco/).
- **Maximum Total:** Annual (1.6%) + Banked (if applicable).
- **Unit 1 Estimate (Annual only):** $3,995 × 1.6% = **$63.92** → New Rent **$4,058.92** [EXECUTIVE-SUMMARY.md].
- **Unit 2 Estimate (Annual + Banked 6 years):** 1.6% + (6 × annual rates) ≈ **13.1%** → **$412.65** → New Rent **$3,562.65** [EXECUTIVE-SUMMARY.md].
- **Links:** [SF Rent Board Rates](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-francisco/) | [571 Allowable Annual Increases PDF](https://www.sf.gov/documents/34344/571_Allowable_Annual_Increases_26-27_1.pdf)

### State (California) — Displaced for Cap [EXECUTIVE-SUMMARY.md]
- **AB 1482:** 5% + CPI (max 10%). **Does not apply** to SF units covered by stricter local ordinance (Civ. Code §1947.12(d)(3)) [EXECUTIVE-SUMMARY.md].
- **Notice Periods:** 30 days (≤10%) / 90 days (>10%) [EXECUTIVE-SUMMARY.md].
- **Anti-Retaliation:** Civ. Code §1942.5 [EXECUTIVE-SUMMARY.md].
- **Link:** [California Legislative Information](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/) (Search Civ. Code §1947.12)

### Federal — No Cap [EXECUTIVE-SUMMARY.md]
- **Fair Housing Act (42 U.S.C. §3604):** Bars discrimination based on race, color, religion, sex, familial status, disability [EXECUTIVE-SUMMARY.md].
- **Link:** [42 U.S.C. §3604](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2023-title42/html/USCODE-2023-title42-chap45-subchapI-sec3604.htm)

## 4. Corporate vs. Individual Handling (Verified Sources)

- **Big Real Estate Corporations (REITs/Institutions):**
  - Must comply with the same SF Rent Ordinance §37.15 licensing and 1.6% cap [2](https://www.bancalsf.com/sf-rent-board-housing-inventory) [6](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-francisco/).
  - **Algorithmic Pricing Ban:** SF §37.10C prohibits algorithmic rent-setting [EXECUTIVE-SUMMARY.md].
  - **Verified Source:** [SF Rent Board Housing Inventory](https://www.sf.gov/rent-board-housing-inventory) (Applies to all owners).
  - **Unverified/Flagged:** Specific internal corporate behavior data is not published in official .gov sources [EXECUTIVE-SUMMARY.md (FLAG-08)].

- **Individual Property Owners:**
  - Identical legal requirements: Housing Inventory report → License → Notice [4](https://www.sf.gov/rent-board-housing-inventory).
  - **No exemption** for individual owners [4](https://www.sf.gov/rent-board-housing-inventory).
  - **Verified Source:** [SF Rent Board - Learn about rent increases](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-francisco/)

## 5. Correct Notification and Reasonable Reasons

### Correct Way to Inform [6](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-francisco/)
1. **Written Notice:** Must state dollar amount, percentage, effective date.
2. **Breakdown:** Annual vs. Banked portions.
3. **Delivery:** 30/90 days in advance (+5 if mailed).
4. **License Check:** Confirm license is active on effective date [2](https://www.bancalsf.com/sf-rent-board-housing-inventory).
5. **Link:** [SF Rent Board Notice Guide](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-francisco/)

### Reasonable Reasons [EXECUTIVE-SUMMARY.md]
- **Lawful:** Published annual adjustment (1.6%), banked prior years, documented O&M petition, certified capital improvement, utility passthrough.
- **Unlawful:** Market rent, tenancy length, new occupant, repair complaint, wanting tenant to leave.
- **Link:** [SF Rent Board - Landlord Petitions](https://www.sf.gov/landlord-petitions-and-passthroughs)

## 7. One Unit vs. Both Units (Discrimination Analysis)

- **Recommendation:** **Raise both units** under a single written policy, applying each unit's specific lawful maximum (not necessarily the same percentage) [EXECUTIVE-SUMMARY.md].
- **Reasoning:**
  - **Discrimination Law (42 U.S.C. §3604):** Focuses on the *basis* (protected characteristic) rather than matching numbers [EXECUTIVE-SUMMARY.md].
  - **Consistency:** Applying each tenancy's own lawful maximum (Unit 1: 1.6% or 3.0%; Unit 2: 13.1%) under one rule avoids arbitrary treatment [EXECUTIVE-SUMMARY.md].
  - **Risk:** Raising only one unit could trigger scrutiny if the basis appears discriminatory (e.g., tenant characteristics) rather than tenancy history (banked years) [EXECUTIVE-SUMMARY.md].
- **Link:** [42 U.S.C. §3604 (Federal)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2023-title42/html/USCODE-2023-title42-chap45-subchapI-sec3604.htm) | [SF Rent Board - Discrimination](https://www.sf.gov/departments--rent-board/)
